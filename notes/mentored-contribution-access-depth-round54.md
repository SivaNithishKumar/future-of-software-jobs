# LFX mentorship: a real mechanism, with a closed published intake and unresolved terms

LFX/CNCF documents a structured contribution route with project approval, mentors, applicant selection, evaluations and conditional stipends. Its accepted 2026 Term3 list includes a concrete Apicurio Registry MCP-validation project. **The published application window ended 18 August.** This is not an open place or payment offer today. Non-student access is plausible under the retrieved general rules, but current binding eligibility and the full terms are not established in this bounded pass.

The question and census were fixed before calls. GSoC/NumFOCUS,NLnet,Prototype,fellowships and nf-core were already covered and were not counted again. Exactly three official surfaces were targeted: LF's mentees documentation,CNCF's programme README,and one term/project README. No fourth eligibility,stipend,IP/AI or application page was opened. The two README pages are moving `main` references;no immutable source-byte/commit match is claimed.

## Eligibility is a conditional route

The **indexed** version of the same [LF mentees page](https://docs.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/mentorship/mentees) lists an age 18 minimum,local work eligibility,no conflicting third-party obligations,no previous LF mentorship,project prerequisites and limits on substantial prior involvement with the target project. It does not list student enrolment or a degree. That permits a conditional inference for a professional who is new to that project;it is not a universal waiver or an assessment of the reader.

There is a source-version gap:the current direct text projection contains benefits/payment material but omits that indexed eligibility section. Removal,unchanged current policy and the cause of the difference are all unproved. A separate eligibility destination appeared in discovery but was not adopted as a fourth surface. Personal residence,rights,prior participation and employment/IP obligations remain unknown;no private contract was inspected. More prior target-project contributions are not automatically better under the indexed rule—do not turn programme preparation into qualification farming.

The direct LF text says mentees are not LF employees. Stipends depend on progress/evaluation,with successful completion for the final installment; a satisfactory evaluation precedes an Expensify report. That supports a genuine conditional support mechanism,not a salary. Amount,residence formula,tax,fees,net cash and any personal funding reservation remain unverified. Dedicated stipend terms were not opened;GSoC or NLnet amounts/rules cannot fill the gap.

## Selection, rights and preparation gates

CNCF distinguishes maintainer approval,mentor confirmation and administrator approval of a **programme** from mentors selecting **mentees** for technical fit and collaboration. Interviews and a Statement of Purpose are described;the prose mentions it both while applying and after selection,so exact step order remains unresolved without the form. Public listings or project approval do not confer personal permission or selection probability. The guidelines route public questions to the mentoring discussions board and explicitly discourage direct messaging of maintainers/mentors. No channel,profile,form or account was used.

IP,project licence/DCO/CLA,employer conflicts and AI-use/application-authorship rules were not established by these three pages. Working on AI/MCP is not permission to submit AI-generated prose/code. Public access is not a licence grant. Not being an LF employee does not settle tax,work authorization or another contract's obligations. The next factual gate would be exact operative programme/project terms and an eligible dated intake—not a message or application triggered by this report.

**No existing artifact or cap is attached.** The considered evaluation146 contract supplies related oracle discipline,but its 25–50 deliberate scorer cases and 32–48 FIRST/96–144 TOTAL planning range do not cover a Java/Quarkus registry API/UI integration or full-time programme obligations. Those old hours are not borrowed,and no new course,exercise,hours or canonical project is invented. A local proof remains distinct from mentor-approved work;previous work is not assumed payable.

Hold programme-specific investment while current eligibility,intake,IP/AI/payment conditions and actual access remain open. Existing transferable software work can retain its own purpose. Acceptance,completion,mentorship quality,upstream contribution and payment are different events;selected recipients do not estimate a causal training return or a 2031 employment path. This is a source-only access audit,not a personal rejection,application recommendation or final freshness check.
