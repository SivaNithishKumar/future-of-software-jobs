## What the positive result does and does not test

An experimental reporter assay is meaningful functional evidence. It can measure sequence perturbation effects **inside its assay context**. Prediction–measurement agreement is stronger than a model grading its own proposed mechanism. It still differs from issuing predictions first and validating new wet-lab outcomes, reproducing endogenous regulation, predicting a whole individual’s phenotype or improving patient outcomes. This report offers no clinical guidance or diagnosis.

A variant’s experimental label, reference interval, cell context and downstream calibration exposure are separate axes. Do not transfer the track model’s out-of-fold condition to the all-fold variant model. Conversely, an all-fold reference model does not by itself show that reporter labels leaked into training. Actual variant/label/sequence intersections require the unreturned detailed methods and selected manifests. A public historical benchmark’s original challenge setting also cannot silently become a newly blinded prospective test for a later model.

The LASSO comparisons matter. Ordinary supervised feature aggregation and scorer/cell-track choices can contribute to a gain; a stronger foundation model is not the only explanation. “Cell-agnostic features” is not the same claim as held-out, unseen-cell biology. Pair-specific comparator coverage also means the denominator must travel with the score. Mean correlation is neither per-variant calibrated uncertainty nor a clinically actionable probability. Full methods could support stronger or narrower holdouts; that remains open rather than a finding of failure.
