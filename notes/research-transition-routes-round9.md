# Funded research transitions: eight additional mechanisms

Checked **20 October 2026**. This is a bounded audit of access mechanisms, not a directory of prestigious organisations, a ranking, an application recommendation or an estimate of admission odds. The accompanying [structured records](research-transition-routes-round9.json) contain eligibility, dated funding, public channels, source limitations and preparation joins. User degree, age, citizenship, work rights, student status, existing research/OSS experience, technical depth and relocation preferences remain unknown.

The useful distinction is **what the funding buys and which gate comes first**. A short mentored fellowship, permanent engineering employment with development, stipend-supported OSS contribution and a four-year doctorate create different obligations and evidence. A scholarship available only after PhD enrolment does not solve entry into research. No inspected source establishes that one route is best for this user or guarantees a job in 2031.

## What each mechanism adds

**IIT Madras: a faculty bridge with a currency problem.** The [current WSAI page](https://wsai.iitm.ac.in/fellowships/post-baccalaureate-fellowships/) was readable through its official search index, while direct retrieval returned 403. The [RBCDSAI FAQ](https://rbcdsai.iitm.ac.in/work-with-us/post-baccalaureate-fellowships/) mixes older age and stipend blocks. It supports a historical funded mechanism, including onsite research, but its INR 50,000/month block cannot establish 2026 pay. Resolve the current cutoff date, intake, supervisor availability and contract before treating this as accessible. A small, careful replication aligned with an actual group is more portable than spending months optimising for an unverified cohort.

**TCS/IISc: support after doctoral entry.** The [IISc Cycle 20 page](https://odaa.iisc.ac.in/tcs-phd-research-scholar-program/) and [issuer flyer](https://odaa.iisc.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/TCS-Research-Scholar-Program-Flyer.pdf) describe an enrolled-scholar award across computing-related research, with a TCS mentor. Its application closed in January. Current displayed staged monthly funding is recorded in JSON with its cycle; it is not a salary available to a software engineer who has yet to enter a doctorate. The preparation implication is to justify the research/credential route first, then assess institutional funding. Entering a PhD only to chase this particular award would invert the dependency.

**STFC: engineering employment can be research-adjacent training.** The [official graduate page](https://stfccareers.co.uk/graduates/) describes a two-year development programme on a permanent contract, technical pathways including computing, mentoring and a departmental placement. It announces 2027 opening; the linked vacancy portal did not expose a readable specific role. Salary, site, sponsorship and actual computing duties remain unresolved. This mechanism challenges the assumption that scientific credibility always starts with a doctorate: operating reliable scientific software can itself be supervised domain experience. The inference is conditional on an accessible role with suitable work, not the programme brand.

**Bristol: funded doctoral entry with separate decisions.** The new [Practice-Oriented AI CDT](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/cdt/practice-oriented-ai/) accepts 2027 applications. Its [application requirements](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/cdt/practice-oriented-ai/apply/) ask for both coding/AI evidence and an evaluated case study; an academic offer is distinct from an award. The [funding page](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/cdt/practice-oriented-ai/funding/) quotes GBP 30,000 for home-eligible 2027/28 entrants with fees/training support, while the limited international-award paragraph still quotes a 2026/27 baseline plus enhancement. Those vintages must remain separate. Funding renewal also depends on progress/registration, and the enhanced home package includes TechFirst activities. Home eligibility is not inferred from nationality. A self-funded offer is not equivalent to this funded route.

**Anthropic: accessible credentials, restrictive geography.** The [live announcement](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/anthropic-fellows-program-2026/) explicitly removes a PhD/prior-publication requirement and describes rolling applications; its 18 October January-cohort priority cutoff has passed. The [exact fellowship posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5183044008) requires fluent Python, full-time availability and location/work authorisation in the US, UK or Canada. It explicitly offers **no fellowship visa sponsorship**. Lower staff boilerplate about sponsorship does not override this. A research-compute allocation is separate from weekly personal stipend. This is not established as a remote-from-India route, even for an excellent engineer; location/rights must be checked before fellowship-specific preparation.

**MATS: a different bridge for existing depth.** The [Residency overview](https://www.matsprogram.org/residency), [track descriptions](https://www.matsprogram.org/residency/tracks) and [application page](https://www.matsprogram.org/residency/apply) establish an open Bridge mechanism, distinct from the [closed shorter winter programme](https://www.matsprogram.org/program/winter-2027). The offer combines salary, research resources and visa support, with substantial in-person time in London/Berkeley/DC. It does not require a PhD, but expects high-quality prior work and a credible research plan. Bridge is for bringing hard-won expertise into safety research; advisers provide resources rather than daily line supervision. A 30–50-hour diagnostic can reveal fit, but cannot manufacture that experience. Salary ranges and support ceilings do not establish an individual offer or immigration approval.

**GSoC/NumFOCUS: bounded mentored contribution, with an unusual eligibility gate.** The [Google FAQ](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/faq) permits students or open-source beginners; it is not limited to students and is explicitly not employment. An established developer cannot assume beginner eligibility. The [2026 schedule](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/timeline) is closed, [stipends](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/help/student-stipends) depend on project size and residence, and [NumFOCUS's confirmed 2026 record](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2026/organizations/numfocus) creates a genuine scientific-computing mentoring link. Neither its future selection nor a particular library's 2027 project is verified. Use the [public contributor guidance](https://github.com/numfocus/gsoc/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING-contributors.md) to understand the mechanism; passing evaluations, code adoption and employment are separate outcomes.

**CERN: practical supervised work, not general graduate entry.** The [exact IT/Mathematics/Robotics posting](https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/CERN/744000141918950-technical-studentship-it-mathematics-robotics-2027-1) is expired after 16 October. It offers CHF 3,486/month with insurance deduction and supervised technical work, but requires continuing full-time non-PhD student status, prior study and specific nationality eligibility. The posting's temporary nationality exclusions must not be replaced by a generic description of CERN's international community. A future cycle could be useful to an eligible student; a graduate already outside enrolment cannot assume access. No inference about the user's nationality or visa is made.

## Conditional preparation from the shared foundation

These are **alternative uses of the existing preparation DAG**, not eight new curricula or additive deadlines. The [DAG](preparation-dependencies-round8.json) stays frozen at SHA-256 `2e7b654bd255d18f61d211a97bbdd88e28b9342bd595c8b28bc0c7bb47cc64e3`. Its first 30–50 and next 90–150 focused hours are planning assumptions, not measured time-to-admission. A fresh diagnostic may justify skipping elementary study. Route-specific work starts only after the eligibility screen produces a plausible path.

1. **Production/scientific-software branch:** STFC, eligible CERN and eligible GSoC projects can value a real bug reproducer, a data/quantity contract, an independently specified result and maintainable tooling. Choose one real system and a modest task. A generated fixture that merely mirrors the importer is weak evidence. An independent reviewer should provide a new failure case and check the actual path; scientific consistency still does not prove a detector, robot or instrument works physically.

2. **ML/research-engineering branch:** IITM, Anthropic and some Bristol projects can share one carefully bounded evaluation artifact: baseline, frozen split, resource denominator, uncertainty and a negative case. It should answer a question meaningful to the chosen group rather than display maximum model complexity. New credentials, expensive compute or speculative hardware are not the first diagnostic. Fluency and method understanding must survive a fresh task prepared independently of the learner.

3. **Research-method/credential branch:** Bristol provides graduate entry; TCS is downstream support; MATS requires a distinct argument for a substantial expertise-based research transition. The relevant evidence is an answerable question, methods appropriate to that question, a result that could disconfirm it and attribution of prior contribution. A doctorate is justified by desired research methods, supervision or access, not a claim that all engineering work will require one.

No candidate who authored or inspected the final test answers has an unseen learning holdout. A known task can still be useful practice or an openly labelled study-policy diagnostic; do not pretend otherwise. A separate expert also needs to audit scorer validity. A technically impressive artifact cannot waive age, degree, nationality, enrolment, work-right or funding gates.

For year-one evidence, favour one attributable, reproducible contribution and an independently reviewed explanation of limits. In years two–three, responsibility for releases, data quality, a research subsystem or a repeatable collaboration is stronger than a growing certificate count. Years four–five leadership would require evidence of judgment, adoption, mentoring and sustained responsibility in the chosen setting. These are conditional development hypotheses; no programme source establishes a promotion schedule or guarantees downstream demand.
