## Unresolved methods and claims

The abstract cannot establish randomization unit/strata/allocation, balance, participant selection/geography/stage, baseline AI access versus actual use, exposure dates, outcome windows, blinded scoring, self-report validation, attrition/missingness, primary outcomes, multiplicity adjustment or prespecified subgroup analysis. Registration timing, version and match to this experiment remain unknown.

The1.9times revenue headline needs the actual definition, currency/period, zeros, raw/mean/log/asinh treatment, retransformation, tail treatment/winsorization and uncertainty. A mean effect, a transformed-outcome coefficient and an upper-quantile effect are different quantities. None was reconstructed or rejected here. Reported upper-tail concentration makes this a priority check rather than proof that the result is spurious.

Capital/labor **demand** must not be silently rewritten as realized financing, headcount, hours, payroll, layoffs or occupational vacancies. It is unknown whether these are intentions, forecast needs or observed inputs, and whether the paper’s null is precise enough to rule out economically relevant effects. Unknown precision is not evidence of no effect. Treatment ITT, treatment-on-treated/IV estimates and any mediation claim must remain distinct; neither the coefficient type nor exclusion assumptions have been verified.

Preserve the positive result as a serious opposing/qualifying lead: an active information intervention reportedly improved short-run firm outcomes despite shared AI access. It can motivate testing workflow discovery against a competent comparator. It does not yet prove a universally effective90-minute intervention, permanent human mapping scarcity, lower realized capital use, no employment effect or a2031 developer job forecast. This checkpoint neither dismisses the benefits nor upgrades their methodological status.
