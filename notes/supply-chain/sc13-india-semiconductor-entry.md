# SC13 — India semiconductor software entry through institutions

The useful distinction is organizational tool access versus an individual opportunity. Checked at the 21 October 2026 India cut; no citizenship, degree or institutional affiliation is assumed for you.

The [ChipIN institutional request procedure](https://c2s.gov.in/request_for_EDA_Tool.jsp) includes Indian academic/domestic R&D organizations outside financially supported C2S projects. Relevant degree programmes, labs and permanent VLSI staff are required. Organization heads nominate two PoCs and provide undertakings. Availability is licence-dependent and access can be withdrawn; use is for teaching/instruction/research. Vendor EULAs and organization onboarding precede service access. Listed users include students, faculty, researchers and professionals/engineers at that organization. Training/reporting targets are obligations,not observed graduates. The undated page’s invitation is not verified live capacity. Domestic startup/MSME applicants are referred to a separate DLI route,whose terms were not reopened here.

The [project-proposal eligibility page](https://www.c2s.gov.in/eligibility.jsp) instead calls for existing infrastructure and regular CI/Co-CI faculty with VLSI experience. Listed institution categories, additional private-institution programme/accreditation branches, evidence and committee approval apply. Submission is through the CI. Its “proposed” eligibility wording does not establish a selected institution, stipend or a general individual degree/citizenship rule. Programme availability and personal admission/employment remain separate.

The [regional-support call](https://c2s.gov.in/call_for_proposal_RSCs.jsp) specifies a 23 July 2026 deadline, already past at this cut. It targets financially supported C2S institutions in their own region; consortium participation is excluded. Hosts provide space,connectivity and backed-up power without extra cost; PoCs are regular teaching/research faculty. ChipIN controls staff recruitment/deputation. Installation, licence checkout, invocation, design-flow support and escalation are defined work. RSCs facilitate but cannot approve/allocate/modify tool access or licences. The initial term is one year,extension conditional. This is a real programme-defined software-support function,not an observed individual vacancy, contract payment or automatic receiving route. Later extensions, selections and current recruitment were not checked.

A non-obvious consequence is that central support and regional facilitation are different authorities. Holding a public procedure, attending training or reproducing an open flow would not make someone an authorized vendor-tool user or programme approver. Conversely, these pages do not establish that every individual needs a PhD or that all access requires a funded project. The institutional programme/curriculum conditions must not be converted into a citizenship rule or a degree waiver for the reader.
