# SC11 — selected HBM, tool/software, U.S.-person and FDP layers

Read 20 October 2026. This closes selected published-text questions, **not the complete current-law gap** from SC07. Different dates, objects and predicates must remain separate.

The main source is [FR Doc 2024-28270](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-12-05/pdf/2024-28270.pdf), published 5 December 2024 and effective 2 December. Its opening dates assign **31 December 2024 compliance** to specified HBM/SME/FDP/DRAM amendments; other instructions have their own timing. Publication, effectiveness and compliance are not interchangeable.

| Layer in that published instrument | What is actually specified | Transfer that fails |
|---|---|---|
| HBM item, ECCN 3A090.c | Memory bandwidth density **strictly >2 GB/s/mm²**; packaged-device bandwidth and package area where applicable | Total system bandwidth, marketing “HBM”, or an accelerator licence alone determines this item |
| Conditional HBM exception, §740.25 | A separate **strictly <3.3 GB/s/mm²** ceiling plus exporter/parent, purchase, packaging and other conditions | Every item below 3.3 is unrestricted; the ceiling itself grants eligibility |

| Specific software entries | 3D992.b multi-die floorplanning plus die/package co-design/co-simulation; 3D993 includes multipatterning, computational lithography and specified equipment-throughput software | One GAAFET software entry exhausts EDA controls; every generic compiler/optimizer is covered |
| Software access, §734.19 | Covered use/renewal keys follow the underlying authorization; existing corresponding authorization can cover the key | Access keys are automatically outside controls, or every renewal needs a second identical permission |
| SME/FN5 foreign direct products | Specified product scope **and** destination or FN5 end-user/transaction nexus, with knowledge and other conditions | A foreign product is controlled solely because some firm is American, or foreign fabrication removes every US nexus |

The HBM technical note also distinguishes processing-dominant logic/HBM co-packages from HBM attached to an interface/PHY. Exclusion from **this entry** is not exclusion from all controls. The note is not a generation-name classifier. The software throughput provision is specifically **>1% wafers/hour**, averaged over any time interval for identified equipment—not general AI efficiency. Operative clauses and exact locators are in the source companion.

The rule’s specified de-minimis provisions have no threshold for certain equipment containing specified US-origin ICs and the stated destination/entity nexuses, subject to exclusions. That is materially different from a universal “zero US content” rule or a global ban. This report does not enumerate exclusions as procurement routes.

## Why an activity layer also matters

[FR Doc 2023-23055](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-10-25/html/2023-23055.htm), published 25 October 2023 and generally effective 17 November 2023, supplies selected §744.6 activity context. Authorizing delivery, delivering and servicing described items **not subject to the EAR** can still fall within specified U.S.-person controls. Its advanced-node and unknown-node provisions have different knowledge, facility and headquarters/parent conditions. The 2024 instrument separately revises the SME product scope, including specified software/technology parameters.

The 2023 text also retains scoped administrative/clerical and otherwise-excluded information/software provisions. Neither being a programmer nor putting material on a public repository determines the outcome. The statutory person definition, all current parent clauses and subsequent amendments were not completely audited. A temporary instruction’s printed end date does not expire the whole rule. The reader’s person, employer, citizenship, location or work authorization was not classified.
