# EDA and open digital-hardware software: option audit, round 11

Checked 19 October 2026 UTC; draft, not a runnable implementation. `compute-hardware-round3.md`, `formal-verification-option-round11.md`, the taxonomy and source index were read first. Existing coverage concerns accelerator/runtime economics and specification fidelity; it does not supply a practical digital-design toolchain/PPA entry route. This pass covers that gap through two current papers, mature source/tool artifacts and narrowly selected official work routes. No software installation, third-party execution, clone, bulk artifact/model download, device purchase, private access, submission or outreach occurred.

## Finding

An experienced developer can enter semiconductor work through **EDA software, simulator/compiler integration and reproducible engineering infrastructure** without owning a board or designing transistors. This is a substantial systems specialization: C++/Rust/Python, algorithms, HDL semantics, timing constraints and reproducible measurement matter. It is not necessary to begin with materials physics or a fabrication company; physical-design sign-off and novel CAD algorithms require deeper domain expertise than a short Python benchmark exercise.

AI already automates EDA script generation, parameter search and changes to placement/resizing code. The useful portfolio is therefore a correct, maintainable tool integration with defensible constraints and measured tradeoffs. A claim that low current agent scores guarantee a lasting human scarcity premium is not supported. By 2031 these roles could emphasize tool/runtime ownership and cross-stage diagnosis, while improved automation could also reduce manual tuning work inside existing teams.

## What each outcome actually establishes

| Outcome | Evidence available without a board | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| RTL simulation | Declared HDL behavior passes an independent testbench/reference under exact simulator settings. | Finite tests are not complete correctness; reset, unknown values, scheduling, CDC and analog effects need separate treatment. |
| Synthesis | RTL maps into the selected library; cell/net counts and estimated combinational/sequential structure are available. | Synthesis area is not final die area or routed timing. |
| Placement/routing and static timing | A physical model produces routed geometry, extracted/modelled parasitics and timing/PPA reports under declared constraints/corners. | Estimates are model-dependent. DRC checks geometry; LVS/connectivity and functional behavior are distinct checks. |
| Tapeout | A foundry-facing design package is submitted under its process/program requirements. | It does not establish fabricated, working, qualified or economically useful silicon. |
| Silicon and customer operation | Requires fabricated devices, bring-up, measurement, packaging/test and operating evidence. | No result here establishes measured joules, yield, reliability, customer utilization or paid demand. |

This distinction is especially concrete for Nangate45: the checked OpenROAD library is expressly **non-manufacturable**, although its files have an open license. A complete educational RTL-to-GDS flow on it is useful toolchain evidence and cannot be a tapeout result. ## Two current research anchors, including contrary evidence

**AuDoPEDA — actual EDA-code automation.** Amur Ghose, Junyeong Jang, Andrew B. Kahng and Jakang Lee, *Automated QoR improvement in OpenROAD with coding agents*, [arXiv 2601.06268v2, 9 February 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.06268v2), first submitted 9 January; paper CC BY 4.0. It modifies detailed/global placement and resizing, evaluating multiple designs/PDKs. Maximum reported routed-wirelength and effective-period reductions are 5.9% and 10.0%. Its newer power experiment reports up to 19.4% lower estimated power **with effective-period increases of roughly 1–10%**; this is a tradeoff, not universal improvement in every PPA dimension. Selected wirelength tests disable equivalence checking. Experiments used 112 vCPUs/220 GB RAM; that is an experimental host, not a proved minimum for a tiny design. The [August 20 v1.0 artifact, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22020200](https://zenodo.org/records/22020200), advertises patches/plans/results in a 381.7-kB archive; it was not downloaded, and complete run-matched environment/rights were not validated. The result is meaningful counterevidence to treating CAD-code modification as exclusively human work, not evidence of chip-product revenue or developer job counts.

**PostEDA-Bench — a closure/evaluation surface.** Pengju Liu, Nuo Xu, Jinwei Tang, Yu Cao and Caiwen Ding, [arXiv 2605.06936v4, 7 October 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06936v4), first submitted 7 May; paper CC BY 4.0. It has 145 DRC/PPA tasks. The **v4 full text**, rather than a stale abstract/search summary, reports nine models and best practical DRC success 54.67%; the PPA multi-objective best remains 20%. Five repeats and task-level uncertainty are described. Released tasks can be scored with open tools, although commercial tools generated some starting layouts. Current v4 qualifies PPA behavior checks as applicable testbench checks and adds a post-hoc connectivity/device audit: no connectivity/device changes among 648 audited DRC successes. Preserve that counterevidence; do not equate DRC success with an established broken circuit. The benchmark uses predictive ASAP7 and report-derived PPA, not fabricated chips. Extra reasoning calls/flow executions differ across scaffolds, so success versus total compute/tool cost remains the meaningful comparison. Source-level limits below matter more than a broad proof-fidelity resurvey.

## Three measurement boundaries a developer can own

**Simulator semantics versus physical timing.** Verilator is mostly two-state; reset/unknown-value behavior can differ from a four-state simulator. Its HDL timing option supports scheduling controls, but `specify` timing checks are ignored. Therefore a simulator accepting `--timing` is not static timing closure or proof that setup/hold constraints are met. Current cocotb source documents simulator minimum versions and changed evaluator behavior; simulator/version differences should be test dimensions rather than hand-waved compatibility. [Pinned Verilator language documentation](https://github.com/verilator/verilator/blob/60f166e1ef2a52ddbf46df0c5bd88783bd1f4a2b/docs/guide/languages.rst), [pinned cocotb simulator support](https://github.com/cocotb/cocotb/blob/76f10e34b76da430a81e3e313965b2fd01bc9e48/docs/source/simulator_support.rst).

**Physical reports versus constrained utility.** Freeze RTL behavior, clock/latency/throughput, library/corner, I/O delays/load, clock uncertainty, false/multicycle paths, extraction stage, density and resource settings. Record setup and hold slack, unconstrained endpoints, routed DRCs, cell/core/die area, wirelength and run cost. Compare like stages: synthesis estimates and routed parasitics are different. State units explicitly; no global assumption that every technology/report uses the same time or power unit. One attempted 6.7-MB Liberty-file range retrieval returned empty content, so its units were not independently inspected here.

Power is an estimate under a declared activity model. Use the same stimulus, accepted transactions, clock, voltage, temperature, activity window and annotation coverage; separate idle, switching and leakage. SAIF/VCD availability does not mean every net was annotated, and RTL activity may not include gate-level glitches. If only vectorless defaults are available, report them as such and stop short of workload-energy claims. The ratio of lower power to a slower clock is not automatically lower energy per completed operation. AuDoPEDA's power/timing tradeoff is a concrete reason to require a fixed timing floor.

**Benchmark convenience checks versus complete behavioral accounting.** At [`posteda-bench@75037635f7c4496e76eac3edef4bef61d8ec6c9a`](https://github.com/pengjas/posteda-bench/tree/75037635f7c4496e76eac3edef4bef61d8ec6c9a), root CC BY 4.0 does not erase upstream design/IP grants. Its [PPA scorer](https://github.com/pengjas/posteda-bench/blob/75037635f7c4496e76eac3edef4bef61d8ec6c9a/eval/ppa_score.py#L66) correctly requires every declared target and fails missing final metrics. Its [functional sanity script](https://github.com/pengjas/posteda-bench/blob/75037635f7c4496e76eac3edef4bef61d8ec6c9a/eval/equiv/check_equiv.sh#L37) explicitly skips designs without compatible testbenches; in event mode it compares a shared prefix after tolerating some event-count differences. Its [latency comparator](https://github.com/pengjas/posteda-bench/blob/75037635f7c4496e76eac3edef4bef61d8ec6c9a/eval/equiv/compare.py#L50) tests overlap under shifted timing. These are disclosed bounded checks, not full equivalence. An independent queue/transaction ledger must additionally reject dropped/duplicated/reordered events and unapproved latency changes. This pass did not execute the benchmark or establish that any reported score exploited these limits.

## No-board experiment: a constrained RTL-to-physical report pipeline

One main project, **40 working hours over 30 days and 120 total over 90 days**, conditional on setup approval and actual feasibility. It is a toolchain/integration probe, not a claim of novel chip architecture or a guaranteed completion schedule.

**First eight hours — launch gate.** Write a self-authored synchronous, ready/valid FIFO contract: depth four, 16-bit words, declared reset behavior, no loss/reordering/duplication, fixed capacity and minimum throughput/maximum latency under declared backpressure conditions. Define the independent Python queue reference and test cases before implementation. Freeze a single flow and its submodules/container digest, simulator/cocotb pair, platform files and licenses; estimate disk/RAM/runtime for this small design. No board is needed. If an approved runtime or consistent tool/PDK views cannot be obtained within eight hours, finish the source/configuration/report-schema audit and label all HDL/PPA execution pending. Do not silently replace missing physical results with fabricated metric JSON.

**Rest of the first month — one correct baseline plus measurement.** After approved tool access, simulate self-authored RTL against the queue reference with reset, empty/full, wraparound, simultaneous enqueue/dequeue, long stalls and independently generated transactions. Use deterministic seeds and at least 100,000 accepted transactions across these scenarios, with per-transaction IDs; this is a planned finite test budget, not proof of all inputs. Exercise a second available simulator for reset/unknown/scheduling-sensitive cases. Require all accepted inputs and outputs to be accounted for and explicit deadlock/latency deadlines. Demonstrate that deliberate drop/duplicate/reorder mutations are rejected by the independent checker.

Map/place/route the baseline on the pinned educational Nangate45 platform with a separately declared **1-ns clock target**, fixed I/O assumptions and one documented timing corner. This is an illustrative test constraint, not a silicon performance promise. Run one comparable implementation change—such as a mux/storage arrangement—while preserving depth, interface/latency and throughput. Retain the baseline and failed candidates. Primary acceptance: correctness unchanged; no unexplained unconstrained endpoints; setup and hold meet the same declared constraints; no new routed violations; all area/timing values have stage, unit and source report provenance. If physical-design setup is too large, stopping at simulation plus a real limitation report is the correct state.

**By 90 days — a bounded design-space experiment.** Reserve one wider/deeper FIFO configuration and one independent small handshake block before tuning. Compare defaults, a predeclared grid/random search and optional AI-assisted proposals under the same maximum **12 exploratory flow attempts per arm, including failures/timeouts**, CPU/thread and timeout budgets. One possible bounded search is `CORE_UTILIZATION` in {45,55,65} and `PLACE_DENSITY_LB_ADDON` in {0.10,0.20,0.30}, after confirming their meaning at the chosen flow pin; these are explicitly permitted floorplan/search variables, not edits to functional or timing requirements. Keep the original behavioral and timing constraints immutable; attempted relaxations are reported and rejected. After selecting one candidate, budget at most six additional final attempts per held-out configuration: baseline and candidate at the same three placement seeds where supported. Otherwise characterize repeatability under fixed seeds/thread count and do not invent stochastic confidence. Include model/token/review cost for an approved AI arm. Promote candidates using validation cases, then evaluate each untouched configuration in that final paired phase without further tuning.

Report the constrained Pareto set rather than one composite winning number: cell/core/die area, setup/hold slack, routed wirelength/violations, elapsed/CPU time, peak memory, failed-flow count, and estimated power only with its actual activity assumptions. Require a proposed PPA gain to exceed observed run variation while preserving every hard gate. If no such gain appears, a reproducible negative result and useful parser/runtime diagnostic are valid deliverables. No requirement to generate a fabricated neural/AI win exists.

**Kill or narrow** when a missing library/corner or stale report cannot be resolved; fewer outputs are mistaken for correctness; a candidate wins by changing clock/I/O/latency/capacity; synthesis/P&R metrics are mixed; a power result lacks activity provenance; or setup exceeds budget. A tool-algorithm improvement is not ready for a broad upstream PPA claim after only these toy blocks: OpenROAD's contributor guidance asks for many tests and evidence that a gain is not design-specific. Optional PicoRV32 work begins only after this pipeline is sound and exact transformed-IP/configuration rights and semantics are checked. An ISA compliance suite/compiler download would be a separate approved dependency, not already available proof.

## Prerequisites and official work/contribution routes

Minimum foundations: digital combinational/sequential logic; clocks/reset and blocking/nonblocking HDL assignments; bit widths/signedness; ready/valid protocols; basic architecture; independent test design; graph algorithms/optimization; C++/Python/Linux and build/debug/profiling. Add Liberty/LEF/DEF/SDC literacy, setup/hold slack, PVT/corners and parasitic modelling for physical-design tooling. Compiler/runtime work adds IRs, code generation, linking/ABI and simulator interfaces. Device physics, analog design and foundry qualification are separate deeper routes. Learn the specific missing fundamentals through this project rather than treating a whole new degree as the default prerequisite.

## Interpretation for 2031 and remaining checks

The defensible option is to become a systems engineer who can make hardware-design software correct, usable and measurable, then deepen into simulation/compiler internals or physical optimization. The rival is that better agents automate much of the coding and tuning; AuDoPEDA already makes that rival concrete. A durable signal is a maintained regression, diagnosed cross-tool discrepancy, real constrained PPA comparison or an upstream change others adopt. A screenshot of routed GDS or a short course certificate alone does not establish those skills.

Unresolved before execution: compatible complete tool/container/PDK lock; prepared library/corner and generated-view provenance; optional Kepler terms if used; actual host resources; stock/transformed design rights; activity-annotation support; full AuDoPEDA artifact/environment terms; and role-specific work authorization. The large Liberty range read returned no text and was excluded from source-inspected counts. All projects remain proposed. Companion records validate existing-node attachments, source pins and explicit proof/rights boundaries; original reports/taxonomy/index were not edited by this agent.

Validation: the UTF-8 text of all **33 inspected source files** independently reproduces its returned Git blob SHA-1 using the standard Git blob header. This verifies the source bytes read, not a compatible executable stack or paper reproduction. Three independently authored arithmetic/sequence fixtures illustrate prefix loss, a fixed timing floor and unit conversion; no benchmark code, HDL simulator, synthesis, place/route or timing tool was executed.
