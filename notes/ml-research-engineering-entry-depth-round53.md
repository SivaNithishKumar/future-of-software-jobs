## Where integration, science and ownership differ

The already-held Anthropic FDE example concerns accepted customer deployments, production Python/agents and enterprise discovery, with its old experience-text conflict, travel and geographic gates. A good evaluation component can help that work without demonstrating customer delivery. It is a historical comparison here, not a newly refreshed third vacancy.

The two new RE titles themselves cross infrastructure and novel-method work. They do not establish a universal boundary between engineer and scientist. No new foundation-model scientist posting was inspected, so a universal PhD/publication rule would be invented. A small scorer/reproduction report does not prove novel architectures, optimization/post-training research, large-model training ownership or training-data governance. Other employers' scientific/robotics PhD requirements cannot be imported as a general law.

A stronger original contribution needs an actual result, primary prior-art comparison, a credible baseline/holdout and independent technical review. An accepted maintained contribution and sustained operational/researcher use can provide further evidence. Those are separate gates from a course, a certificate, a title or the passing of 90 days. No acceptance, learner assessment or promotion is claimed.

The practical decision is conditional. Resolve current role and location/equivalence access before costly role-specific preparation. If the relevant depth already exists, organize existing evidence rather than prescribe more study. If it does not, use the single bounded proof only for the layer it can test; ordinary software/platform work remains a legitimate adjacent path. Failure of an access gate or an imprecise result is not evidence of personal failure. No individual hiring odds, wage return or 2031 scarcity follows from these two texts.
