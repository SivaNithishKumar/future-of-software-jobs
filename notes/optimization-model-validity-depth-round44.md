## Positive protocol and what its scalar means — O44-P

The paper reports 561 expert-reviewed tasks with conventional/expert references, hidden target techniques and reference solutions. Independent reviewers examine statement clarity and references; repairs trigger affected checks. Valid transformations may change variables/feasible sets if justified and recover requested decisions; duality/decomposition have explicit conditions. Numerical accuracy instead checks OPTIMAL plus a finite reference-matching objective at 1e-6 tolerances. Cost comparisons condition on correct, measured runs within a 543-task reference subset; shifted geometric ratios are not total-time ratios. Timing separates build, tracked solves and final generation; it is not a complete retry/repair request bill. [Definitions 1–2, §4.1, Appendices D–I](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38884v1).

These are meaningful positive controls. The authors explicitly state the boundary between numerical agreement and full task validity; this pass does not discover an unacknowledged solver-pass guarantee. Their paired conventional/expert comparison also matters: the repository reports all evaluated models ahead of the conventional solver-time reference under its aggregate, while expert references remain stronger. That supports useful automation as well as improvement opportunities; it does not prove either general autonomous correctness or a permanent human advantage. [Official reported findings](https://github.com/ZhongLIFR/EfficientOpt).

## Four units of evaluation to keep separate

**1. Business statement → agreed task.** A benchmark can specify a clean objective/domain/output contract. A real buyer may have unstated qualifications, calendars, priorities, policy exceptions or data errors. An omitted business requirement cannot be found merely by checking the generated model against an equally incomplete statement. Before an operational claim, the domain owner must approve what is included/excluded and what an unknown requirement does. This is a missing external oracle, not evidence that every model is wrong or humans are irreplaceable.

**2. Task → mathematical procedure.** Correctness belongs to original requested decisions and objective, not syntactic resemblance to the reference. Auxiliary variables, projection, duality and decomposition can be valid alternatives. A checker that demands the reference's variable names or identical feasible-set representation would reject legitimate automation. Conversely, one coincidentally matching objective on one fixed instance cannot establish equivalence for perturbed data, return a missing action, or justify an unproven stopping rule. Feasibility, objective recomputation, bounds/optimality and required decision recovery must be separately named.

**3. Procedure → executed program.** Solver status certifies something about the submitted formulation under its numerical settings; it does not identify every natural-language constraint. Record statuses, tolerances, decisions, residual/constraint violations and objective in original units. A zero-duration/no-op, unchanged incumbent or missing optional action needs an explicit legitimate/invalid/unknown classification. Do not discard failed, unsupported or timed-out candidates from an all-attempt quality denominator merely because they cannot enter a conditional speed comparison.

**4. Program → paid decision outcome.** Solver work units, externally timed calls, building/preprocessing, model-generation latency, tokens, memory snapshots, recovery and human review are different measurements. A smaller formulation is not necessarily faster. Equally, a faster solver run can be useful even if it is not cheaper end to end. Paired eligible results answer a narrower question than mean cost across all offered work; missing costs stay unknown. Operational ROI additionally requires a current workflow comparator, integration/support costs and an actual accepted decision/outcome. This research has none.

## Leakage, curation and all-attempt boundaries

The construction protocol begins with selected existing problem sources and redesigns tasks around modeling techniques. The hidden technique/reference interface is a real control, not permission to send ground-truth paths or review records to a candidate. The repository warns to keep technique-coded directories and reference material out of generation prompts. That control should survive any wrapper. [Repository interface](https://github.com/ZhongLIFR/EfficientOpt).

Independently approved future evaluation should group original problem family, source/template, reformulation ancestry and scaffold/prompt exposure, separating tuning from fresh problem families and data perturbations. Hiding a technique name does not by itself establish train-test novelty; conversely, no actual training contamination or scaffold leakage was established here. The benchmark's curation/repair effort is not a per-task production human-labor requirement. Selected clear solvable tasks also do not identify the all-arrival rate of ambiguous business requests.

Retain the study's reported failure/outcome categories and its distinction between all-task accuracy and conditional cost analysis. Report a second expected-cost/recovery table if the proposed workflow needs all-attempt cost. Do not silently turn a conditional speed or technique-attribution label into operational acceptance, general formal correctness, or a ranking across different success subsets. This note did not recalculate published scores or audit raw experiment records.
