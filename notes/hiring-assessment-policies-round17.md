# Candidate AI use and technical assessment: six official employer examples

20 October 2026 local research date; source receipts retain actual UTC read times. These six purposeful examples establish **different assessment contracts**, including within the same employer. They support practicing independent reasoning, assisted engineering and explanation as separate capabilities. They do not establish which interview format selects better employees, national hiring prevalence, an individual's chance of an offer, or a stable 2031 interview standard.

The sample uses Anthropic and Monzo from earlier role research, adding Canva, Datadog, Microsoft and OpenAI for explicit contrasts. GitLab's proposal is a separate screened example, **outside the six**. This is software-heavy policy sampling, not a representative employer survey. No role snapshot was refreshed. No application, form, login, interview or assessment was attempted; no private question bank, candidate record, model call, installation or outreach was used.

“No AI” does not automatically settle whether documentation, internet search, an ordinary IDE or a particular accommodation is permitted. “AI allowed” does not automatically allow an undisclosed answer service, external human assistance, confidential-data uploads or use in later rounds. Only the applicable stage's explicit instructions settle those details; none were requested under this research task.

## A proposal is not an operational interview

The official [GitLab AI-Native Hiring Working Group](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/company/working-groups/ai-native-hiring/) page still labels its status **Proposed**, with a 4 April 2026 start, 15 May target end and 15 April last modification (`3a272159`). It proposes asynchronous MR review, synchronous design/iteration and existing-codebase work with Duo, including temporary equal-access tooling. The page explicitly requires piloting, validation and a later approval/rollout decision. A target date passing does not establish deployment. This is useful design evidence and a tooling-access question; it cannot be counted as a seventh enacted policy or proof that candidates currently receive Duo.

## Practice links without adding another curriculum

These are **conditional practice designs**, inferred from the published rubrics and linked to existing artifacts. They are not measured interventions that improve offer rates. Choose one relevant project; the four modes below are slices of that project, not four projects. Existing role degree, experience, language, work-rights, location and security constraints remain separate.

| Mode and existing proof | First-month slice | Conditional deepening around day 90 | What the evidence cannot replace |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Independent diagnosis:** round 16 learning diagnostic; round 14 retained-skill proof; round 9 contract | Define a narrow invariant and expected result; perform fresh same-concept and changed-condition checks under explicit resource rules. Separate tool-free implementation from documentation-allowed debugging. | Repeat on a genuinely new failure, recording intervening practice and exposure. Explain why the expected result follows. | Employer threshold, unobserved skill, degree/equivalence or work rights. Self-authored answer keys do not create a blind test. |
| **Assisted workflow:** round 14 workflow/evaluation; round 9 delivery | Clarify one ambiguous requirement, delegate a bounded subtask, inspect changes, reject a flawed suggestion and verify against an independent oracle. Retain actual tool/version/time and assistance provenance. | Change the requirement in the same artifact; show a regression test and what automation helped or missed. | Permission for a real assessment, independent competence, a paid subscription or employer-provided access. |
| **Review and defense:** round 9 review/maintenance; round 14 data proof | Write a concise review identifying a correctness issue, test gap and relevant trade-off. Explain the decision without a generated live answer. | Show a changed-condition repair and remaining limitations; independently supplied review remains an access gate. | Staff-level organizational influence, production reliability or correctness inferred only from a fluent explanation. |
| **Contribution/accountability:** round 9 contract/enablement; round 14 workflow | Give an evidence-linked account of personal versus team contribution, shipped versus pending impact and tool assistance. Use public/synthetic material for practice. | Update only with actual new maintenance/review/impact evidence; make an understandable handoff. | Fabricated business outcomes, permission to disclose private work, leadership title or placement/earnings return. |

The optional round 16 diagnostic already proposes **30–45 focused hours in month one plus 40–70 through day 90**. Embed relevant modes inside that cap if selected. The separate preparation route menu uses 30–50 hours followed conditionally by an additional 90–150; those are alternatives, not additive commitments or guaranteed 30/90-day completions. Hours remain planning assumptions, and personal availability is unknown. No learner assessment or practice execution occurred.

The delayed-transfer evidence does not justify replacing fundamentals with assisted rehearsal. Conversely, an independent exercise alone does not demonstrate competent AI-assisted operation. Keep the outcomes separate: correct execution, fresh diagnosis, code comprehension, assistance decisions, explanation and provenance. If the learner authored or saw all holdout answers, label the result an unblinded diagnostic. A single passing visible example or eloquent explanation is not a sufficient oracle.

## Inference and revision limits

Published policy is an employer statement of desired conduct and evidence, not causal evidence of selection quality. Neither Canva's pilot impressions nor Anthropic's team experience supplies an audited comparison showing which preparation strategy increases hiring or subsequent employee performance. No pass-rate denominator, applicant composition, rejected-candidate outcomes or country weighting was established. Two employers having similar prohibitions are not two independent estimates of a national trend.

Revise the relevant card if a later explicit policy or actual candidate instruction changes its stage permission, a supposed introduced format proves only proposed, or a dated blog is superseded. Revise practice if independent diagnosis fails despite polished assisted output, the oracle is exposed/invalid, or tool/accommodation access is unavailable. These findings narrow what an artifact establishes; they do not diagnose personal inability. A policy contradiction, missing date, blocked fetch or changed URL must retain its exact provenance rather than become evidence of employment decline or a prediction about 2031.
