## Observed positive protocol — G42-P

The v1 uses 127,155 exported pretraining instances from 150,000 selected locations, each 24 monthly timesteps, 96×96 pixels on a 10 m grid. Inputs include resampled Sentinel data, coarser environmental products and model-derived Dynamic World/WorldCereal maps. It evaluates frozen kNN/linear probes separately from fine-tuning, reruns competing models and selects hyperparameters on validation sets. GeoBench splits are inherited; Brittany holds out a different NUTS-3 region, while PASTIS uses declared folds/monthly aggregation. Base performs strongly in aggregate, without winning every task. Pretraining Table 11 reports 200/259/573 H100-hours for Nano/Tiny/Base 500-epoch runs; learning-rate/weight-decay sweeps are additional context, not a complete deployment cost. [Methods §§2–4, Appendix B and C, Tables 11–14](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.09356v1).

This is positive evidence for useful reusable representations, including a meaningful regional holdout and attention to comparator tuning. It would be wrong to erase these controls or call every published test a random-patch split. The evidence also does not turn a held-out benchmark into an as-issued decision or prove every new geographic/temporal regime.

## The unclosed joins

**Time has several meanings.** A 24-step sequence or seasonal input is not a record of what a customer could receive before a decision. A later-produced embedding can legitimately describe earlier imagery retrospectively; that does not establish contemporary availability. This pass did not resolve an exact pretraining acquisition interval, model/checkpoint training cutoff, per-product publication/revision times or consumer receipts. No claim that the whole paper lacks those details is made. For an in-season/disaster claim, monthly aggregations also need a cutoff rule: a completed month cannot automatically stand in for the prefix available earlier in that month.

**Patch, scene, region and year are different independence units.** Preserve the native benchmark splits as reported. A stronger proposed experiment would join patch footprints to parent scene/acquisition and group overlapping or adjacent footprints, repeated parcels and years. It would separately disclose exposure to unlabeled pretraining imagery. Such exposure is not automatically supervised label leakage; conversely, a new downstream label split does not establish never-seen-location pretraining. The actual full training/evaluation footprint intersection was not computed, and no overlap is alleged.

**A common grid is not a common measurement.** Resampled grid spacing, native sensor support, acquisition footprint, nodata and cloud rules must remain separate. A coarse environmental value repeated over a chip does not become a fine-scale observation. A map-derived input is a prediction; if evaluation labels share its producer or upstream observations, independent truth needs a separate lineage check. This is a dependency question, not evidence that Galileo's reported labels leaked.

**A frozen encoder still has a downstream learning protocol.** Keep fixed representations plus a trained/selected head separate from full encoder fine-tuning, and separate both from pretraining. Hyperparameter and feature-scaling searches need matching validation access and compute accounting. A cheap classifier is a serious baseline, not an inherently inferior control. For the existing crop project, retain majority/season priors, spectral indices plus logistic/random-forest models and a small frozen-feature head under the same labels/time boundary. The paper's pretrained comparisons alone do not prove equal-total-cost superiority over every such alternative.

**Retrospective maps are not response outcomes.** A benchmark score supplies no measured reduction in flood loss, faster safe dispatch, farm yield, paid analyst hours or deployable profitability. Event coverage, missing/late products, false alarms, selective coverage and decision cost need an independently defined operational target. A regional holdout is encouraging but does not by itself establish climate, sensor, country and future-year robustness simultaneously.
