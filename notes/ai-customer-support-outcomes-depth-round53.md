# Customer-support AI: the worker-level estimand behind productivity and retention

This published study supports a substantial assisted-throughput benefit,especially for newer/lower-performing support workers,under its rollout assumptions. It provides more causal structure than a company FTE-equivalence claim. It does not identify wages,jobs,full buyer ROI or durable developer skill.

This is an explicit **follow-up to the same study already audited in learning16**,not another independent workplace trial. That audit examined outages. This pass examines the main rollout,quality,subgroups and worker-retention estimands. Zero new study families are added.

## Source,setting and design

The exact [published QJEarticle](<https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658>),Brynjolfsson,Li andRaymond,*Generative AI at Work*,was published/typeset4February2025 in140(2),889–942. The intervention/data period is2019–2021,not2025or2026. It observes5172agents and3006395chats,mostlyin thePhilippines,for an anonymous business-software firm and subcontractors. This is human-agent assistance:response/document suggestions may be edited or ignored,and low-data topics can receive no suggestion.

The main rollout follows training capacity,manager scheduling and a contract/budget ceiling. Access starts after a three-hour session;ordinary weekly coaching continues. It is not randomized. A small August2020pilot did randomize about half of50workers,but the researchers lacked its actual control identities and compared the treated pilot workers with other untreated agents. That analysis is not a recovered clean pilotRCT.

Preferred main estimates use agent-month outcomes,equal agent-month weighting,time/agent/tenure controls and agent-clustered errors. The reported effect is0.301additional resolutions/hour,SE0.0329,with12295agent-month observations;the paper reports15.2%relative to the pretreatment mean. It is access/activation under difference-in-differences assumptions,not a randomized effect of following each suggestion. Alternative staggered estimators,event studies,clustering/weighting and group-dateIV provide useful robustness. They do not prove parallel trends,noanticipation or exclusion,or eliminate every time-varying selection process. [Main methods and TableII](<https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658>).

## Output,quality and learning differ

The average handle-time and chats/hour effects are positive. Overall resolution-rate andNPS estimates have no significance marker inTableIII;that is not equivalence or a noninferiority test. Lower-skill/novice gains coexist with small quality declines for top groups. Skill is a trailing firm-relative performance index,not general ability or years of software expertise. Assisted experience curves are not unaided transfer tests.

The linked online appendix gives the measurement boundary:resolution is a firm algorithm using chat text and future interactions;NPS uses randomly invited surveys with nonresponse. Minimal exchanges/missing identifiers are excluded,forgotten-open duration iswinsorised,and metrics/tenure use activework periods. These are useful operational measures,not independently adjudicated correctness for every offered case or all paid hours.

Priorlearning16's outage result remains a positive signal consistent with learning through use. Its minutes/hours without recommendations after months of continued exposure are not months without support. That endpoint is not counted again,and its specific preperiod/quality cautions are not automatically transplanted into the main productivity comparison.

## Retention and costs

Attrition is monthly exit,with voluntary and involuntary separation indistinguishable. The appendix reports−0.0868,SE0.0130,over17902agent-months. Treated pre-access observations are removed because those workers must survive to be treated. Agent fixed effects cannot be used for this endpoint;the authors explicitly warn that selecting likely-stayers could overstate it. The novice~10percentage-point/25%baseline example is not an overall employment-growth estimate.

The result is therefore useful retention evidence with weaker identification than the main worker-fixed-effects productivity result. It is not job satisfaction,wage growth,layoffs or a headcount forecast. Individual pay,labour demand,new-hire mix and full implementation/vendor/review/escalation costs are not observed. Manager descriptions of potential performance bonuses are not paid earnings,and targets can change. [Attrition and conclusion](<https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658>).

Two exact appendix-text joins stay unresolved. J.B.3 describes dividing the resolution share by calls/hour,a dimensional tension with the main resolutions/hour definition. TableA.XI's title/columns/specification concern monthly exit without agentFE,while its notes describe productivity and agentFE. Neither observation proves the stored KPI or regressions were computed incorrectly. No operator,score,coefficient or causal result is silently repaired;code/data were not available to this pass.

## What this adds to the career evidence

The study challenges an assumption that AI benefits only experts or cannot improve real operational work. Existing native assistants,documentation,coaching and human teams remain credible baselines. The design does not establish a permanent human moat,a generic chatbot opportunity,an AItraining return or a2031developer-job count.

It also cannot validate Klarna's different automation shares,FTE-equivalence,staffing or savings. Different firms,eras,interventions and outcome units must remain separate. The common lesson is to measure accepted throughput,quality,worker selection,review and full costs together.
