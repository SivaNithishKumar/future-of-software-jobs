## What can and cannot be compared

The [saved HCL bridge](<india-services-filing-bridge-round20.md>) and its independent review supply the baseline. Both firms' total recognizedUSD sales refer to the same September quarter, allowing a limited period/unit comparison. They still combine different businesses, prices, currencies and operating perimeters; neither quantity measures accepted developer output or India-only demand.

HCL'sAdvancedAI quarterly amount and explicit fourfold annualization cannot be treated as the same classification as TCS'sAI-labelled pace without inclusion/allocation rules. The latter is not silently converted to a quarterly recognized number or relabelled anARR. Likewise TCS'sTCV and HCL'snew-deal-winsTCV need a coverage/renewal/cancellation/term bridge before a substantive comparison. Contract value is not current sales, funded seats or a unit of developer work.

HCL'snature-of-expense note separates employee benefits and outsourcing; its external bucket still contains supplier economics rather than pure labor hours. TCS'saccessible functional categories cannot be substituted for that split. The one new arithmetic check is merely4,643+1,166+1,833=7,642. It does not estimate wages, contractor intensity, productivity or a cross-firm cost advantage.

Closing workforce is not an average eligible employee denominator or gross hiring. HCL explicitly excludes involuntary exits and digital-process operations from itsattrition measure; that definition cannot be imported into TCS'sheadlineITServices rate. No layoff count or exits estimate was derived. HCL'sconfirmed in-quarter acquisitions also differ from TCS'sproposed/post-quarter announcements. The available TCSmaterial does not establish acquisition/transferred-worker contributions to the closing stock.

Thus the portable finding is the need to reconcile issuer-specific flows, stocks, rates and transaction timing. It is not a replication of HCL'sexpense movement, evidence of noAI value, or evidence that either employer'sAI growth caused employment or pay changes. One selected second issuer is not an IndiaIT sample.

## One developer implication and a falsifier

For one **existing authorised work result**, attach a short definition/perimeter reconciliation: what was requested, contracted, recognized or actually accepted; which actor/reviewer/external/tool/setup/support burdens are counted; and what changed in version, availability or organizational scope. A competent native alternative may already supply this contract; use it rather than inventing a missing commercial need. This is an evidence improvement to an existing workflow, not a new project,30/90-hour promise or claim of mastery.

Two mechanisms remain compatible with growingAI-labelled business: new integration/deployment/reliability work can be funded, while native autonomy and productivity/pricing commitments can reduce work per accepted unit or reclassify existing work. The public aggregates cannot select between them.

A useful discriminator would be an authorised repeated workflow at a consistent client/perimeter: accepted volume and quality, actual deployment and pricing/renewal terms, full employee/external-review effort, resource/support cost and funded task/staffing decisions, with starts/exits/transfers reconciled. New funded tasks versus fewer fullhumanhours at comparable demand would discriminate locally. A broad causal jobs conclusion needs a credible counterfactual beyond those observations. No private workflow/customer inquiry, hiring scan, trading, account access, outreach, installation or monitoring was performed.

The retained access gap is part of the result. No fourth source endpoint or blocked-route retry was used to manufacture a complete comparison. Existing diagrams, catalogues, indexes, candidate24 and its source/semantic acceptance gates remain untouched.
