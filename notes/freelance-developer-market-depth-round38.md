## What the design resolves and leaves open

**Recorded contracts improve the benchmark bridge.** The observation is closer to paid market activity than an agent solving an old issue. But jobs are placed in their start month and compensation attached to those jobs. A recognized contract, invoice, collected cash and net income are distinct units. Incomplete fixed-price hours, unpaid bidding, repair/support, fees and off-platform work prevent a full personal earnings or margin account. An inference-cost ratio cannot fill those missing quantities.

**Release exposure is not measured adoption.** Freelancer/month fixed effects remove stable person differences and shared monthly shocks. Identification still requires a defensible occupation-specific counterfactual and interference assumptions. A writing-specific post-COVID demand unwind, changed clients/tasks/contract lengths, platform visibility or account selection could produce differential outcomes. These alternatives are not established explanations; they are rival mechanisms that the fixed effects alone do not eliminate.

**Pretrend and robustness checks help without proving the counterfactual.** Event-study captions and95%intervals were read; neither plotted coordinates nor a joint pretrend test were recovered. No visual verdict is claimed. Bootstrap, broader clustering, alternative models, matching and prior-year comparisons probe sensitivities. A shared release and several treated occupations do not become195 independent randomized shocks because the total cluster count is195. A prior-year comparison also changes the macro year and sample.

**Some negative results survive; income magnitude is less invariant.** Jobs remain negative without time-varying controls, while income estimates become less precise. Conditional working-month income has a different selected denominator and is not a zero-effect result. Lagged work/income may themselves respond to the shock; conditioning can change the estimand. The no-controls result is therefore useful counterevidence to presenting one controlled income magnitude as universal. AppendixA must remain attached to the headline.

**The snapshot has a selection boundary.** Private, unsearchable or terminated profiles are absent, and entrants without a completed job are excluded by construction. One later snapshot does not establish random attrition. Profile reputation and posted rates are not repeated measurements; some are only observed after the release. Historical job quality proxies do not constitute randomized training or tested competence. A negative interaction with a reputation proxy cannot establish that acquiring a skill has no return.

**Control contamination has no automatic sign.** AI assistance may raise productivity while a price/demand shift reduces paid contracts. New tasks, new entrants or client diversion may offset or amplify the comparison. Calling a contaminated estimate a lower bound requires additional direction and equilibrium assumptions. The writing and image arms, publisher abstract and AEA version share study/platform dependencies; they are not independent votes. Existing positive Entra task-assistance evidence remains valid for its own accuracy/time outcome and does not identify freelancer income.
