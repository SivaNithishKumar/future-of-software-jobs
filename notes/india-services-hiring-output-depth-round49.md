## What remains undecided

AI-related commercial activity and a small net workforce contraction can coexist under several mechanisms. More accepted work can outweigh fewer hours per unit. A constrained buyer budget or scope can instead convert efficiency into lower hours or prices. Currency, contract mix, utilization, ordinary exits, subcontracting, acquisitions/transfers and consolidation can also move the aggregates. This quarter does not estimate their contributions, identify an AI cause or choose a 2031 staffing path.

The decisive missing bridge is matched client price/quantity/quality and contract model, AI versus ordinary delivery effort, employee **and** contractor inputs, perimeter changes and gross entry/exits. No current role was read: corporate revenue and workforce aggregates cannot establish degree equivalence, experience, work rights, location, sponsorship, pay or individual hiring odds. Reskilling language cannot establish independent competence or the causal return to a course. The full filing and precise definitions would narrow these gaps; access failure is neither a negative business finding nor proof of zero hiring.
