## Unclosed methods, rather than invented answers

| Needed boundary | What cannot be concluded from this read |
|---|---|
| Independent units |Full physical-cell/replicate/manufacture/test-cohort counts and protocol/chemistry identities; cycles/RPTs are not automatically independent cells.|
| Reference/target |Exact capacity/RPT/resistance and derived degradation-mode measurement/derivation uncertainties; a derived mode is not automatically direct latent-mechanism truth.|

| Holdout |Exact cell/condition/temperature/material holdout and selection units. Absence of a complete audit is not a claim of absent holdouts or leakage.|

| Uncertainty |Appropriate group-level variation, calibration/interval coverage and sensitivity/identifiability analysis.|
| Policy utility |Prospective fleet/maintenance/charging decision loss against a proper comparator; prediction error is not cell safety, life extension or paid value.|
| Complete cost |Physical testing, manual parameterisation, retries, computation, review/support and failed runs.|
