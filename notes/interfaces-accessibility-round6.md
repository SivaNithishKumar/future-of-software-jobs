# Interfaces, accessibility, voice and spatial computing: an outcome-first audit

Research cutoff: 19 October 2026. Round 6, distinct from the earlier general accessibility and Android XR institution notes. Public evidence only; no software installation, model/data download, microphone activation, recruitment, external posting or hardware purchase occurred. This is a research and experiment specification, not a completed prototype or user study.

## Judgment and competing explanations

**Inference:** The defensible developer opportunity is engineering interfaces that let a person complete and recover a real task across keyboard, screen reader, speech and vision. A fluent character, a headset demo, and a low speech-recognition error rate are insufficient evidence of that outcome. For an AI engineer, the useful specialization combines ordinary frontend/mobile engineering, streaming systems, assistive technology semantics, measurement and interaction design. Consumer headset exclusivity is a much riskier five-year commitment than skills that transfer between phone, desktop and wearables.

Three rival models must stay alive:

1. **Native absorption:** Operating systems and foundation-model vendors handle transcription, reading, visual description and basic computer control sufficiently well. The remaining work is product integration and conventional accessibility remediation. This is the default competitor, and could leave little room for an independent voice wrapper.
2. **Workflow and recovery bottleneck:** Recognition improves, but ambiguous instructions, interrupted turns, inaccessible state changes, corrections and uncertainty still prevent completion. Domain-specific state, confirmation rules and observable recovery retain engineering value. This predicts demand for embedded product engineers and evaluation specialists, rather than necessarily many standalone startups.
3. **Embodied interaction bottleneck:** Wearables make some hands-busy workflows materially easier, while weight, battery, sensing and social context prevent generalized replacement of phones. Valuable work concentrates in selected operator workflows and platform teams. A simulation portfolio can teach the software, but cannot establish comfort or field productivity.

Do not collapse these into one market forecast. A useful skill may have few jobs; a real job may pay an ordinary software wage; a valuable integration may have little venture moat. The evidence below supports existing work and testable hypotheses, not a numerical 2031 vacancy or wage prediction.

**Methodological implication:** ASR word error rate, task success, preference, willingness to pay and long-term independence are different outcomes. A model can misrecognize many harmless words yet preserve a command, or transcribe almost everything correctly while reversing a date or negation. Report task-critical slot error and wrong commits alongside WER. A static visual benchmark may miss stale frames, camera movement, a user unable to visually verify an answer and the effort needed to ask a repair question.

There is no universal latency number established by this pass. Measure the whole path: known speech end → endpoint decision → final transcript → proposed action → actual state change → first audible response → correct task completion. A quick filler can improve perceived responsiveness while leaving completion unchanged. Record interruption-to-output-stop separately from legitimate pauses mistaken for interruption. Those are proposed measurement distinctions, not published effect-size claims.

## Native and mature interfaces are the actual competitors

[Windows Voice Access](https://support.microsoft.com/en-au/accessibility/windows/voice-access/use-voice-access-to-control-your-pc-author-text-with-your-voice) already provides on-device recognition and offline PC control/dictation. Availability depends on Windows version, language, supported assets and device configuration. [Apple's current feature directory](https://www.apple.com/accessibility/features/) includes VoiceOver, Voice Control, Reader and Magnifier; supported hardware, OS and language conditions differ. Feature availability is evidence of a baseline, not proof every workflow is accessible or equally good. No speech assets were enabled or downloaded in this research.

**Inference:** For an owned web form, compare an accessible keyboard interface and native assistive technology with finite-command control before adding a conversational model. The product-specific difficulty is often exposing current state, focus, validation, live updates and a reversible action model. If the DOM already contains the exact answer, asking a vision model to guess from pixels can discard a superior information source. Conversely, browser semantics cannot inspect an unlabeled physical object. Choose the source with the most trustworthy task information, and declare what is unavailable.

On-device inference can reduce network dependence and some transmission, but does not inherently solve interaction errors. Cloud processing can offer a stronger model while adding connectivity, data handling and failure dependencies. A fair comparison fixes the task, supported language, model/version and hardware, then measures memory, battery or thermal impact when actual device access exists. An emulator or desktop benchmark cannot supply those missing measurements.

## Hardware and adoption: three distinct products

Camera/audio glasses, display AR glasses, and immersive tracked headsets have different input/output affordances. Sales of the first are not adoption of the third. [EssilorLuxottica's 11 February 2026 FY2025 results](https://www.essilorluxottica.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/q4-full-year-2025-results/) report more than seven million AI-glasses units sold during 2025, including Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta. This is company-reported sales, not active users, retention, third-party developer revenue or task benefit. Its reported group margin was affected by tariffs and AI glasses. Do not infer attractive application economics from unit growth.

[Apple Vision Pro M5 specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/125436) list 750–800 g including the specified seal/band and a separate 353 g battery; vendor estimates are up to 2.5 hours general use and three hours video. These are technical/vendor-test conditions, not a comfort study. Hands, eyes and voice coexist with keyboard/trackpad support. Weight, power and sensing are practical constraints even when a rendering demo is impressive.

There is older operator evidence for bounded workflows. [DHL's 2015 Ricoh/Ubimax pilot](https://www.dhl.com/global-en/delivered/innovation/dhl-successfully-tests-augmented-reality-application-in-warehouse.html) reported a 25% picking-efficiency improvement: ten pickers, over 20,000 items and 9,000 orders in a three-week trial. The [2019 rollout announcement](https://group.dhl.com/en/media-relations/press-releases/2019/dhl-supply-chain-deploys-latest-version-of-smart-glasses-worldwide.html) described deployments after trials in the US, Europe and UK. This is operator/company reporting, not an independently audited randomized TCO result. It demonstrates a workflow preceding generative AI; current device durability, staffing impact and per-site economics remain unverified. Another DHL page's 15% average and this pilot's 25% have different denominators and must not be merged.

**Inference:** Initial competence does not require buying a headset. State machines, timed event replay, spatial coordinate transforms, accessible fallbacks and source freshness can be tested in software. Physical comfort, occlusion, social acceptance, motion-to-photon latency and hand/eye tracking accuracy require later hardware and appropriate participants. Keep those outcomes explicitly unmeasured.

## A no-hardware 30-day experiment: recoverable multimodal task interface

**Question:** Does a model-assisted input adapter improve completion of a small task after corrections and interruptions, relative to accessible text/keyboard and finite-command baselines? The proposed task is an owned, synthetic appointment/task form with fake state changes only. It sends nothing and creates no actual booking. The decisive oracle is its exact final state and permitted transition log, not the model's claim that it succeeded.

**Time/resources:** 35–50 hours across four weeks; existing laptop/browser/editor and already approved dependencies only. Start with text and event fixtures, requiring no audio/model downloads or paid APIs. If an approved model or speech runtime is absent, complete the deterministic and replay tests and label the ASR/model arm unrun. The plan must not silently install a framework. Existing native accessibility/voice tools can be compared only if already available and configured; enabling new downloadable speech assets would require approval.

**Build only the integration:** Use mature UI components and a small explicit action/state schema. Keep keyboard controls available. Each proposed change identifies its source and current values; ambiguous date/name/negation must request clarification. Correction changes the pending value, not a previously confirmed unrelated field. Cancellation stops pending work and output; replays cannot duplicate a commit. These are task requirements, not a claim of a novel general agent architecture.

**Fixtures and baselines:** Predeclare 80 cases: 30 straightforward, 20 corrections/negations, 15 interruption/reconnect/replay cases and 15 missing/contradictory information cases. Hold out 20 across the categories before tuning; those cases must include new combinations rather than mere paraphrases of training examples. Compare (A) keyboard/text form, (B) finite command/slot parser, (C) optional model adapter sharing the same state backend and commit policy. A fourth native-voice path is conditional on existing availability. Do not degrade the baselines with extra confirmations to favor the model.

**Outcomes:** Exact state success; wrong commits per attempted commit; duplicate commits; required versus unnecessary clarification; repair turns; time to correct completion; and failed-task coverage. For replay, log speech-end or synthetic event time, endpoint, transcript-final, proposal, commit, output-start and cancellation. A synthetic clock tests event ordering; it does not establish perceived voice latency. Use accessible role/name/state and keyboard/focus checks on each asynchronous state transition. Manual author testing is n=1 self-evaluation; it is not a substitute for disabled participants or a population usability estimate.

**Acceptance criteria, chosen in advance:** Zero wrong or duplicate commits in the held-out fixture set; at least 90% correct completion on answerable held-out cases; at least 90% correct clarification/abstention on deliberately underdetermined cases; cancel/replay invariants hold in every injected ordering. These are project engineering gates, not externally validated safety thresholds. Print counts and confidence intervals where meaningful; twenty held-out cases cannot establish a rare-error rate. Compare time only among correctly completed tasks and include repair effort. Document all failures, including a model losing to the parser.

**Kill criteria:** Stop the model arm if it adds errors or materially increases repair/confirmation cost without a predeclared benefit. Stop the standalone-product thesis if native tools already complete the target task adequately. An attractive demo and all-green generated fixtures do not establish hiring demand or a buyer. The learning artifact remains useful if it honestly explains why the simpler interface wins.

## A 90-day extension: choose one branch, not all three

Budget an additional 70–110 hours. These estimates assume the 30-day foundation works; lacking an approved runtime or licensed fixture source is a real dependency. Keep a frozen baseline, versioned fixture manifest, error taxonomy and rerun instructions.

1. **Streaming repair:** With already available or later-approved small licensed audio fixtures, compare an existing framework's turn/cancel behavior to a narrowly changed recovery policy. Keep the recognizer/model/voice/content fixed when varying endpoint delay; change one factor at a time. Test unfinished turns, long pauses, partial transcript replacement, disconnect and double delivery. Evaluate critical-slot errors, false endpoints, late cancellations, task recovery and p50/p95 completion delay. Repeated self-runs quantify repeatability for one operator, not population preference. Stop if improvements vanish against the framework's current documented configuration or require unreliable inferred emotion. Public read-speech fixtures cannot validate conversational accent fairness.
2. **Nonvisual evidence and verification:** Create 60 owned synthetic documents/images and matching structured truth, including cropped numbers, absent labels, contradictory/stale frames and values deliberately unreadable from pixels. Compare direct structured extraction, an available OCR route and an optional multimodal model. Measure exact field accuracy, unsupported assertion rate, appropriate abstention, question usefulness and recoverability after a corrected source. Keep screenshot variants of the same document together in a split. No real financial/medical/identity documents or others' recordings. Author review can validate factual outputs, but cannot prove blind users find the interaction usable. Stop if exact extraction dominates or a model merely abstains on everything.
3. **Spatial input/fallback:** Use an already available browser/runtime and synthetic pose/event traces to test loss of tracking, stale coordinates, hand/controller switching and transition to keyboard/2D controls. Check target identity, coordinate-frame consistency, focus recovery and duplicate actions. A prerecorded trace gives a precise software oracle. CPU frame times and headless tests do not establish device frame rate, motion-to-photon latency, comfort or accessible embodied use. Stop any claim of hardware advantage without later device evidence. This is an integration portfolio, not a headset product validation.

For each branch, the strongest original contribution could be a minimal regression case, a clear timing/state schema, a documented native/framework limitation or a carefully bounded negative result. Preparation is local; publishing a repository, issue, benchmark or paper remains a separate action. A credible portfolio explains whose task was tested, its rights and measurement limits, how the mature baseline behaved, and what remains unproved.

## Position by 2031: milestones conditional on evidence

**Inference, not a job forecast:** In year one, become strong at accessible UI state, streaming-event debugging and reproducible task evaluation. In years two–three, seek ownership of a real deployment where completion, repair effort, support burden and retention can be measured with authorized users. In years four–five, technical leadership means selecting appropriate modalities, conducting or partnering on valid human evaluation, managing device/platform constraints and deciding when a model is unnecessary. A publication or open contribution can demonstrate a specific competence; neither alone establishes broad leadership.

The hypothesis weakens if native platforms remove the measured recovery gap, if users prefer a simpler interface, if implementation support costs exceed workflow value, or if eligible employers do not hire this combined skill set. Track actual role requirements, deployable workflow outcomes and repeat use, rather than counting launch announcements. Research-scientist, product-engineer, accessibility-specialist and spatial-runtime routes have different credential, geography and equipment barriers; choose through demonstrated work and available access.

## Source method and limits

Methods: official current documentation; primary papers and author full texts; operator and issuer statements explicitly labeled company-reported; direct job-page state checks; public GitHub metadata and package-level LICENSE files through read-only `gh api`; an indexed official procurement notice where direct access failed. No social sentiment, job-board aggregates or vendor TAM forecasts are used as outcome proof. Current-source checks occurred on the stated cutoff; role listings and SDK behavior can change.

Known missing evidence: representative 2026 Indian-accent conversational comparisons; independent long-term wearable retention and per-workflow TCO; accessible task outcomes from representative disabled participants; a full public reproduction package for several human studies; current India vacancy verification; sponsorship eligibility; wage scarcity and a causal link between model improvements and role counts. These gaps constrain the confidence of the career inference; they are not filled by fluent demonstrations or historical studies.
