Our interpretation and attempts to disprove it:

- A greater engineering *share* does not establish more engineer positions. Reject that bridge unless absolute roles, firm entry/survival, geography and attainable requirements are observed.
- A description classifier and a named-tool dictionary are noisy measures of different things. Failure of the dictionary to explain an association cannot establish that internal AI use is ineffective. A stronger rival comparison would audit actual process/tool use under the same observation window.
- A count of occupied seniority categories can change without changing actual reporting layers. Keep organizational-level claims tied to that operational measure. A direct reporting-network sample would test whether the interpretation survives.
- Market valuation per matched employee is not operating productivity, revenue, profit, paid customer retention or total human effort. Denominator matching and missing valuations require separate sensitivity checks. Do not turn it into a project success metric.
- Similar estimates across count sources challenge an argument that the entire pattern is a single-source accident. They do not remove selection, timing or classification uncertainty. Preserve this counterevidence to an overbroad critique.
- A contemporary AI label applied to historical headcounts could mix selection and changes of identity. No timestamped label snapshot was established in this bounded inspection; treat that as an open timing question, not proof of leakage.

Actionable consequence: a preparation project can demonstrate an accepted product/workflow outcome and the engineering judgment behind it. It cannot use this paper to promise abundant roles, a valuation, or automatic advancement. For a future venture experiment, count all human review/support, model costs and failed attempts against one independently checked customer outcome; a small founder team alone is not success. Those future experiments remain proposals under the existing selection policy, with no outreach or implementation authorized by this note.
