# Six source-bound AI-use pathways, round 22

These six alternatives connect **local evidence preparation, community contribution and technical assessment as separate gates**. They are not six curricula, employer referrals or employment pipelines. The employer examples compare assessment contracts; nothing here establishes that a community feeds that employer, that the employer has a suitable vacancy, or that a project meets its degree, experience, geography or work-right requirements.

The [structured mapping](<ai-use-pathway-policy-round22.json>) retains exact project/policy occurrences, file and canonical-value hashes, public source URLs, policy dates/pins, stage rules and unknowns. It uses the frozen channel16, channel26, independent channel21 and hiring-assessment17 records. No new primary scan, private fact, learner assessment, project execution, install, login, message, upstream draft, agreement or submission occurred.

“Human-owned code” here means understanding the design, explaining and independently checking it, and accepting maintenance responsibility. It is not a legal determination of copyright ownership. A human-understood contribution can be AI-assisted; a polished AI artifact is not by itself evidence of independent skill, novelty or actual impact.

## Permission and evidence do not transfer

An AI-assisted code policy does not settle issue/PR prose or autonomous submission. A community's rules do not settle an employer's rules, and general candidate guidance does not settle every team/stage. Conversely, a no-AI assessment does not automatically prohibit documentation, an ordinary IDE or an agreed accommodation; an AI-allowed stage does not authorize any outside assistance, undisclosed service or use in later rounds. The actual assigned contract must resolve those questions before a real assessment.

Local preparation now means original contract/case design and reading the retained sources. It does not mean that native runtimes are installed or that the reader's understanding has been tested. Keep the original project's rights, first-fixture, oracle, resource, hour and kill gates; choose at most one relevant option. The six routes add no new time budget. A self-authored answer key is exposed and cannot be called an unseen retained-skill test.

Before a later upstream action, a human needs actual evidence, understanding, current applicable policy, artifact rights and any required agreement/sign-off; the user must explicitly authorize the external action. Before an assessment, the actual team's/stage's instructions must specify permitted AI, documentation/search, tools, provenance and accommodation. This report asks for neither action and drafts no message, application, issue, PR, commit or reviewer response.

A known-baseline reproduction, useful regression, accepted maintained contribution and original research are different claims. Report actual personal/team/tool contribution and execution honestly; do not invent novel results, shipped impact, public acceptance or unassisted authorship. No policy-efficacy, retention, hiring, employee-performance or earnings study was conducted. Published employer rubrics describe intended evidence, not validated returns to following this map. The separately screened GitLab AI-native hiring page remains Proposed, not an enacted seventh assessment or tool entitlement.
