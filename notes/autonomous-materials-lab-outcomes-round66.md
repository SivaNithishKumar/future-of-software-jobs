## What remains open

A scientifically adequate phase audit needs matched diffraction, competing structural models, composition/reference conventions and qualified independent interpretation. A novelty audit additionally needs a dated, appropriately licensed literature/structure search. Neither follows from being absent from model training. A useful-property or commercial claim needs its own measured endpoint.

An autonomy audit needs every offered target and attempted recipe, errors/retries, instrument availability, manual interventions and attention. Human initialization and after-the-fact interpretation must be recorded rather than treated as either zero work or hidden misconduct. Aggregate success does not give an all-attempt intervention rate. Economic value requires an equally observed conventional workflow and personnel, setup, maintenance, instrument, consumable and compute costs; no such estimate is established here.
