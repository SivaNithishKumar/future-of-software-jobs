# Bastani methods follow-up: immediate independence is not delayed transfer

**This is a methods follow-up to an already-cited trial, not a new independent study.** The question was fixed before retrieval: what do the unaided exam’s clock, task distance and control conditions establish, and what does the safeguard contrast isolate? Learning34, education5, Trio27, delayed-design/thesis/outage16, prerequisite plans and role gates were censused first. Their original findings and access gaps remain separate.

## The endpoint is now narrower and better supported

The readable [68-page author-hosted manuscript](https://hamsabastani.github.io/education_llm.pdf) places lecture, practice, common solution review and closed-book/closed-laptop examination inside each90-minute session. Exam problems are conceptually very similar to corresponding practice problems; the material was previously covered. AppendixA.5 explicitly describes independent blinded graders. Four sessions are exposure, not a post-withdrawal delay. The authors defer long-term learning to future work (PDFP4:L97–99).

The main survey sample is839;943 includes honors. Table1’s2,848 observations are student-sessions. Main inference clusters by classroom; class assignment uses scheduling/balance-constrained optimization and school-randomized class formation. ITT retains five noncompliant class sessions. AppendixB.6 reports12.3% average absenteeism and no detected differential attendance. These are useful controls, not zero attrition or an independent-student allocation.

GPT Tutor bundles hint constraints with teacher-provided solutions, common mistakes/feedback and additional welcome prompts. Its large assisted-practice benefit is real within the reported endpoint, but does not isolate a cheap generic prompt or prove delayed learning. Its immediate exam estimate is close to control and nonsignificant; this is not a prespecified equivalence or noninferiority result.

The [directly read registration](https://aspredicted.org/4DL_Q3J),#144093, dated18September2023, names the short-term pen-and-paper quiz and three pairwise t-tests. The paper separately reports covariate-adjusted,class-clustered regression and the registered comparisons. Those specifications must not be silently relabelled identical. The [August2025 correction](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2518204122) was directly inaccessible; indexed official text concerns Osbert Bastani’s affiliation, not an outcome change. No second correction/body route was attempted.

## What follows—and what does not

The study can distinguish successful assisted practice from subsequent independent performance under a common classroom schedule. Paper examination, support withdrawal and blinded scoring make its immediate endpoint stronger than satisfaction or another assisted score. Its result should survive that qualification; narrowing the duration is not dismissing the experiment.

The comparison does not establish week/month retention after assistance ends. A four-session programme is not four sessions of time without support. Nor is a teacher-designed, closely matched math problem evidence of unfamiliar software repair, broad scientific reasoning, general expertise or job readiness. Shared solution review before the quiz is part of the learning environment, not an absent intervention to be imagined away.

Scheduled classroom time is common, but equal minutes do not prove equal active effort, error exposure or attention. A learner can obtain an answer quickly, ask for hints, check an attempt or disengage. Those pathways are possible mediators, not separately randomized teaching policies. The tutor’s expert answer/error content and feedback timing also differ from a bare hint instruction; the experiment cannot price those components or identify which one accounts for the contrast.

A nonsignificant tutor-versus-control exam result leaves benefit and harm uncertainty. It does not justify either “guided AI teaches nothing” or “guardrails guarantee independence.” No new significance or equivalence test is calculated here. Three planned pairwise comparisons and the different reported regression need their own inference conventions; the registration does not specify a multiplicity procedure. The paper’s exploratory mechanism, performance-dispersion and error analyses should not be promoted to randomized mechanisms.

## Version, reproducibility and rights

The author file’s observed cover is undated and lacks a final PNAS imprint. Selected methods/appendix pages were inspected, not every one of its68 pages. Its main estimates agree with the retrieved indexed publication text; this does not establish byte identity to the corrected journal supplement. The correction’s limited stated scope should neither be turned into a methods scandal nor used to certify every version.

The registration declares no data collected at filing and describes a school-sized sample rather than a power-derived target. It is direct prespecification evidence, not a reconstruction of actual recruitment. Survey exclusions, honors scope, absence and repeated observations need their own denominators. Detection of no differential attendance is not proof that all missingness is harmless. The author appendix also reports sensitivity including non-survey responders; no participant data or analysis code was acquired or rerun.

The public-repository availability pointer was observed but not opened. Exact code/data licences, model checkpoint/API version, dependencies and raw record access remain unverified. Article/correction reuse terms are separate from assessment items and learner logs. No published question or answer is repurposed as a fresh test; no participant records, account, model or software was acquired.

## Existing preparation context only

The reference remains `education-quantum-or-round7.project.learning-diagnostic`, exact original`/records/178`: **30–45FIRST hours and70–115TOTAL conditional hours**, with its existing4–6-hour launch gate. The seven-day slot, independently unseen forms, executable result oracle/explanation rubric and equal-study-time worked-example/retrieval comparator already exist. This follow-up adds no worksheet, task, question, key, schedule, hours or learner assessment.

Existing oracles/measurement prerequisite modules can use the source distinction: record assistance availability, last practice, common feedback, task distance and assessment support separately. That is a reporting relationship, not proof the modules are completed, that the user lacks skill or that a personal diagnostic estimates a population treatment effect. Without an independent unseen form, retain the unblinded-practice label. Native/reference adequacy, a faulty oracle or exposed keys can invalidate a learning inference without invalidating useful assisted work.

A new controlled delayed study could test a different clock and task distance. This trial cannot answer that question by repetition or a stronger headline. It supports preserving useful assisted delivery while separately measuring independent capability; it supplies no universally best policy, AI ban, causal training return, hiring effect or five-year mastery forecast.
