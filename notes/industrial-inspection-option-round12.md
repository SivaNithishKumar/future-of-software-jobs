## Finding and 2031 implication

Industrial inspection is a credible software route through **decision reliability, integration and measurement evidence**. A public anomaly heatmap is a weak standalone business claim. Existing suppliers already offer model training, ordinary vision, interfaces and application engineering. The defensible problem is a particular accepted production decision: which defect matters, what dimensions and uncertainty are acceptable, how often good parts are rejected, whether the correct physical part is diverted, and who maintains the system when parts or imaging change.

Inference through 2031: engineers who combine Python/C++ vision with statistics, image formation, line timing and maintainable software have a clearer route than someone selling a generic anomaly-model wrapper. This is conditional on access to an actual quality owner and line; neither benchmark ranking nor this research establishes available vacancies, buyer budgets, net job growth or profitable startup demand. Model progress can reduce specialist modelling work while increasing the importance of operating constraints. Mature tooling can also absorb the integration opportunity; it is not automatically a new market.

## Three primary anchors, with contrary evidence retained

**MVTec AD 2, current journal version.** [The MVTec AD 2 Dataset: Advanced Scenarios for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-026-02743-0), version of record 9 March 2026, DOI 10.1007/s11263-026-02743-0, adds INP-Former to the older arXiv evaluation. At 256-pixel input it reports 31.6% mean AU-PRO_0.05 for that method. The often quoted “below 60%” concerns AU-PRO_0.30. PatchCore reaches 62.3% AU-PRO_0.05 at half original width/height versus 28.8% at 256; higher resolution costs time and memory. This is a paper’s evaluated snapshot, not an October 2026 leaderboard ceiling. Its article licence is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

The [27 March 2025 arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.21622v1), §§III-B–D, makes the protocol concrete: train/validation are normal and use regular lighting; public test labels are available; private-test **images** remain public while labels are server-held. Mixed private images depict the same scenes with changed lighting. Those paired views are not independent parts. Position and camera parameters, except exposure, stay fixed across the lighting variants. Labels receive on-site annotation, human refinement and manual/automatic checks; normal images are filtered. This is substantive counterevidence to an unsupported “unreliable ground truth” criticism. It still does not establish lot-, time- or camera-heldout operating performance. Hidden labels do not establish absence of public-image pretraining exposure; no such exposure was demonstrated here.

The [official current dataset page](https://www.mvtec.com/research-teaching/datasets/mvtec-ad-2) offers eight scenarios and more than 8,000 high-resolution images, utility code and an evaluation server. Dataset terms are **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, with commercial use expressly prohibited. Download access requests professional details and acceptance. No form was submitted, dataset accepted, utility archive downloaded or server submission made. No immutable data-archive digest was obtained.

**LOCO, useful older protocol still distributed today.** [Beyond Dents and Scratches: Logical Constraints in Unsupervised Anomaly Detection and Localization](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-022-01578-9), 22 February 2022, DOI 10.1007/s11263-022-01578-9, explicitly separates **distinct physical objects** across training, validation and test (§3.1). Preserve that positive evidence; missing lot/camera/time metadata does not prove object leakage. Its logical constraints include missing, extra or inconsistent components. The saturated-PRO metric permits multiple valid localizations for ambiguous logical defects; it is not a dimension-tolerance oracle. Article: CC BY 4.0. The [currently offered dataset](https://www.mvtec.com/research-teaching/datasets/mvtec-loco-ad) is 3,644 images/five categories under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Its external evaluator archive and image data were not fetched or executed.

**Operating counterexample to a universal model opportunity.** BMW’s [2023 AIQX account](https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/news/general/2023/aiqx.html) describes visual/completeness checks and acoustic seat-noise analysis within production workflows. Its [current AI page](https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/innovation/artificial-intelligence.html) continues to describe AIQX line monitoring and real-time image/sensor analysis. This is an operator’s deployment report, stronger than a generic inspection demo. Neither checked page supplies an independently audited denominator for accepted parts, escaped defects, false rejects, uptime, support hours or capital payback. It establishes a bounded use, not quantified factory ROI or an unserved product niche.

## The denominator that changes the business conclusion

A part decision, a pixel localization and a traceable measurement are three different endpoints. An image can contain several parts; a video can contain many frames of one part. Choose the physical decision unit and preserve the mapping before counting errors or throughput. A “defect” in a curated benchmark is not automatically a nonconforming feature according to a buyer’s drawing.

The companion arithmetic fixtures are independently authored hypothetical calculations, not benchmark execution or factory estimates. At 100,000 parts/hour, 100-ppm defect prevalence, 99% sensitivity and 0.1% normal-part false-reject probability, expected hourly totals are 9.9 correctly flagged defects, 0.1 escaped defects and 99.99 rejected good parts. Only about **9.01% of flagged parts are defective**. At 10-ppm prevalence that falls to about **0.98%**. These assumptions say nothing about the cost or danger of a particular defect.

Even 10,000 independent normal parts with zero false rejects give a one-sided 95% binomial upper bound around **299.5 ppm**, not a proven 10-ppm process. Zero failures require at least **299,572 independent normal observations** for that particular upper bound to reach 10 ppm. Correlated lighting variants or repeated frames do not supply that many independent observations; drift further limits extrapolation. These calculations assume fixed thresholds and independent, identically distributed trials.

Report by accepted part: false rejects, escapes by severity/size/location, abstentions/review, uninspected/missing images, retries and duplicates. Report hardware, resolution, memory-bank size, full capture-to-decision latency, p95/p99, deadline misses and queue depth separately from model throughput. Preserve the cost of inspection, review, rework, scrap, line disruption and support. Without buyer-specific costs and independent accepted ground truth, the net-value expression is symbolic: avoided escape/review loss minus false-reject, equipment, integration and operating costs. There is no ROI number to compute from AUROC.

For dimensions, [NIST’s traceability policy](https://www.nist.gov/metrology/metrological-traceability) ties traceability to a **measurement result**, with a documented calibration chain and uncertainty. A calibrated object or instrument alone does not establish the result’s traceability or fitness for a particular tolerance. Synthetic pixels or virtual units cannot support a claim about real millimetres. Later physical work needs a measurand, optics/distortion/lighting/fixturing characterization, a reference, an uncertainty budget and a quality owner’s accepted disposition rule. This report establishes no certification or safety-critical inspection qualification.

## No-new-camera project, with launch gates

### First 8 hours: evidence and resource gate

This is a **proposal**, not an implemented experiment. Allocate at most 40 hours for the 30-day stage and 120 hours total for 90 days. No dependency installation, image dataset, model download or third-party execution occurred during research.

Start with self-authored virtual geometry and tiny lossless grayscale fixtures, avoiding public-data acceptance and weight rights initially. Write one actual image plus its feature/label manifest and read them back before committing to a corpus. The manifest must independently specify part ID, template family, image variant, true hole/component count, dimensions in **virtual units**, defect operation and allowed tolerance. It must include a pass case and a missing-component case. An empty schema is not an oracle.

Make the generator and detector separate implementations; verify several fixtures by hand-computed geometry and a second small raster/measurement calculation. A shared bug in generator and detector invalidates the synthetic result. Decide whether aliasing and subpixel boundaries affect the oracle; exclude ambiguous cases or express their tolerance explicitly.

Preflight intended permissions, exact source/dependency revisions, operating system/backend, one fixture’s shape/range, preprocessing, memory and estimated runtime. Initial ceiling: 512×512 grayscale, <=1,000 training/calibration parts, <=10,000 heldout parts, CPU baseline, <=4 GB process memory and <=2 hours for a complete evaluation on the measured host. These are chosen project limits, not published requirements. Do not purchase compute if they fail; reduce scope transparently or stop.

### By day 30: 40-hour software proof

Use three authored template families (e.g. plate with holes, two-component assembly, label/count layout), normal shape variations and explicit scratches/missing/extra/shifted components. Split **whole part IDs and render seeds** before creating image variants. Keep one template family wholly held out. Keep illumination, blur, geometric nuisance and defect severity ranges recorded. Hold a nuisance regime out separately; do not conflate unseen-template and changed-lighting performance.

Implement the conventional baseline first. Freeze registration, all thresholds, minimum region sizes, allowed component counts and dimensional tolerances using training/calibration only. Reserve final test manifests; do not use final heatmaps to choose preprocessing. Preserve full-field border defects.

Acceptance is deliberately modest: deterministic fixture/oracle agreement; zero cross-split part IDs; fixed threshold; observed false rejects <=1% on heldout normal virtual parts; >=95% sensitivity for each **declared visible synthetic defect class above a predeclared size**, with counts and binomial intervals; and a table of the deliberately difficult near-tolerance/blurred cases. Report every failed render, missing image and abstention. These are educational synthetic targets, not plant specifications.

Measure end-to-end offline parsing/preprocessing/decision latency on the actual host; target p99 <=100 ms at this fixed resolution and disclose missed deadlines. A replayed folder deadline is not physical trigger, camera, PLC or ejector performance. Success proves a reproducible inspection-software harness and an explicit decision contract; it does not prove real optics, factory sensitivity or a novel anomaly method.

### By day 90: 120-hour conditional extension

Only after a rights/access decision, an actual feature/label fixture and a measured resource estimate may a public-data stage begin. The MVTec dataset form, noncommercial use purpose, redistribution rules and archive digest must be resolved first. No form acceptance or benchmark-server upload is authorized by this report. If that gate remains shut, continue self-authored simulation and label the limitation.

For one approved public category, preserve official train/validation/public-test boundaries and derive thresholds only from normal validation. Public test reuse is **development**, not an untouched new confirmatory holdout. A genuinely new heldout camera/lot/time claim requires new lawful data with those IDs; this corpus cannot manufacture it. Separate scene-paired lighting comparisons from independent part counts. Record image-only access versus server-only labels; no local “private accuracy” without the latter.

If a frozen pretrained PatchCore or EfficientAD baseline is added, pin each checkpoint/backbone and its rights and preprocessing before download. Compare against the conventional method at fixed input resolution and a validation-fixed false-reject budget. Cap the first experiment at two hours and the total optional approved compute at eight host-hours; measure memory-bank scaling and memory before extending. No training infrastructure should be built merely to make a portfolio claim.

A useful extension would be an **auditable part-level threshold and provenance report**: immutable calibration statistics, grouped confidence intervals, severity/size slices, drift/refusal states and replayed deadline/missing-frame accounting. Check existing library equivalents before proposing a contribution. The target is a small reproducible improvement or documented failure mode, not a new platform. An ML alternative advances only if it improves predeclared missed-defect performance at the same false-reject constraint without exceeding the measured resource budget; otherwise retain the simpler system and record the negative result.

Kill the metrology claim if physical tolerances/reference/uncertainty are missing. Kill the generalization claim if IDs cannot support its holdout. Kill the ML addition if the conventional baseline already meets the declared task or score normalization depends on future test batches. Kill the standalone product claim if there is no quality owner, legitimate production data, integration access or advantage over existing supported tooling. None of these kill criteria implies that the whole field lacks value.
