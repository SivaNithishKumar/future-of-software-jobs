## Acceptance and full cost use the same planned-task denominator

Report every eligible planned request, attempts, first-attempt acceptance, acceptance after repair, correct abstention, false rejection, unresolved status, timeout and abandonment. Distinguish a legitimate completed effect from HTTP/tool success and from an appropriate refusal. Require exact supported-case agreement with the independent contract; zero forbidden returned fields, wrong/duplicate effects or false success labels in the finite suite; and seeded contract-violating traces to make the oracle reject the broken path. Positive controls must contain an actual forbidden field/effect or wrong success result. Omitting one helper may still be correctly blocked by another layer; retain that protection rather than declaring it defective because an omission failed to cause a violation. A reject-all system fails legitimate completion. Unknown outcomes are reconciled or retained explicitly. A small selected zero-failure suite is not a production error-rate or 99.9% autonomy certificate.

Retain three separate ledgers: active attention by activity/person with overlaps counted once; calendar arrival/queue/review/commit/recovery times; and actual cash/resources with failed/cancelled/replayed usage. Include oracle/case construction, setup, mapping/policy changes, configuration, all attempts, retries, evaluator, repair, support and the later changed-requirement check. Price unknowns stay unknown, not zero. Replay can test a recorded mechanism but is not fresh model behavior or live latency. Keep raw attention minutes before any hypothetical labour valuation; queue waiting is not continuous human labour, and released time is not automatically recoverable cash.

The comparison endpoint is the accepted-effect/attention/resource frontier at the same correctness and severity contract, with utility/coverage and failure counts visible. Predeclare the smallest worthwhile benefit for the selected decision before viewing fresh outcomes; no universal percentage is supplied here. Inaccessible native arms, unmeasured review or missing prices preclude a complete cost-superiority claim. If support/review/recovery erases the custom change's apparent advantage, retain the negative finding and choose the simpler or no-write path. Human oversight and automated verification can themselves improve; neither is assumed to be permanently scarce.
