## Original contribution and accountable work are different next claims

An **accepted contribution to a particular external project** needs a current independently confirmed gap, fit with that project or receiver, review and maintainability. A candidate original engineering or research result can precede adoption; its new contribution, prior art and independent validity still need to be demonstrated. Reproducing a published example is learning. A new regression, source interpretation or integration fix can be useful and attributable without becoming novel science. Conversely, a statistically interesting result can fail a production acceptance contract. The [prior-art audit](<contribution-novelty-round9.md>) already demonstrates this distinction with existing tools and specific maintainer requests; those old issue states are not refreshed assignments for these eight projects. A missing upstream merge or immediate buyer does not by itself refute scientific originality.

Before claiming accountable ownership, each path still needs an authorised receiver, actual resources and decision rights, release/change/recovery expectations, appropriate domain review and evidence of sustained usefulness. A platform absorbing an optimisation can erase a standalone business while leaving meaningful configuration, review, compatibility or maintenance work. That work still needs a funded receiver; technical usefulness, formal liability and larger output do not determine paid seats or wages. Stronger agents may also perform the diagnosis, tests and maintenance, so these gates do not reserve a human bottleneck.

For a longer horizon, ask whether the person can carry accepted work through changed requirements, expose and repair a consequential mistake, explain a competing design, and enable another authorised **human** to work independently. A runbook, assistant-only replay, manager title, GitHub stars or one merged patch cannot demonstrate that human enablement or delegated authority. A five-year window may contain such evidence, an interruption, a different branch or no funded opportunity; none of the sources supplies its probability or automatic timing. Technical leadership can remain an individual-contributor responsibility rather than compulsory management.

## Research-transition mechanisms do not repair unrelated access gaps

The [saved transition routes](<research-transition-routes-round9.md>) offer **possible mechanisms**, not matched groups or offers for these projects. No new vacancy/deadline refresh was performed here.

- STFC's saved 2027 programme is an announced employment mechanism; no readable exact computing vacancy, site or sponsorship decision was established. A weather/software proof cannot manufacture one.
- Bristol's saved doctoral route separates academic and funding offers and has degree/language/fee-status conditions. It may supply supervised methods/access if selected and funded; it is not an automatic next stage for every successful engineering proof.
- Anthropic's saved fellowship does not require a PhD, but has a passed priority cutoff and US/UK/Canada location/work-authorisation limits with no fellowship sponsorship. MATS Bridge instead targets substantial existing expertise and a credible safety-research plan, with different visa support and in-person requirements. Neither turns a general workflow project into an AI-safety research qualification.
- The saved GSoC cycle is closed and student/open-source-beginner eligibility cannot be assumed for an experienced contributor. Evaluations, code adoption, maintainer authority and employment remain distinct.
- IITM's current intake/contract terms were unresolved; TCS/IISc support was a closed cycle downstream of PhD enrolment; the exact CERN studentship had expired and required continuing eligible student status. These cannot be used as generic funding bridges around a project's credential or geography gate.

No PhD is imposed on all software work. Where original research is the intended claim, the router requires one substantive method or equivalent demonstrated depth, not all methods or another automatic curriculum. Where the desired outcome is reliable scientific software, a reproducible maintained contribution with domain collaboration may be the proper target. Whether that distinction fits the user is unassessed.
