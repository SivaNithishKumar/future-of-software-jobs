# Eight conditional ways developer work could be packaged by 2031

Draft for root synthesis and independent challenge, 20 October 2026. **Every 2031 statement below is a scenario or inference.** The anchors are saved 2026 role requirements and audited capabilities/economics. No new source or vacancy scan, personal assessment, implementation or gated freshness recheck was performed.

The practical shift to test is from producing code toward producing **accepted outcomes under a cost and operating contract**. AI can also automate the specifications, integration, tests, evaluation, diagnosis and review needed for those outcomes. Calling them difficult, domain-specific or accountable does not preserve a human staffing ratio. These eight bundles are tasks that can coexist in existing jobs; they are not eight new occupations, independent markets, ranked recommendations or promises of growth.

Three rival conditions apply throughout. **S1, slow adoption:** assistants help, while data, context, procurement and acceptance keep deployment uneven. **S2, consolidation:** native platforms/incumbents absorb more of the loop and a smaller specialist group supports greater volume. **S3, broad autonomy:** strong implementation, diagnosis, evaluation and some research become widely available; cheaper creation also increases supply and competition. Different organizations can occupy different conditions simultaneously. No probabilities are assigned.

This selected set does not cover every researched field. Eight is a synthesis constraint, not an evidence-based boundary around the future job market; important alternative work can sit outside these bundles.

## Choosing among bundles without pretending to know the future

Broad software experience can make a transition cheaper **after** a deep contribution is demonstrated. It does not establish expertise in eight domains. Choose one unknown prerequisite and one existing project. Preserve independent reasoning, assisted operation and explanation as separate evidence; actual interview tool rules vary by stage. A correct native baseline or useful negative result can justify ending custom work.

The JSON preserves the original hour menus: some projects use a 30–50-hour probe with a conditional extension, serving uses its existing 32–48/96–144 planning ranges, weather 25–40 plus 50–90, and inspection/EDA 40/120. Localization and original backend/workflow projects do not have validated personal effort estimates. These alternatives must not be added together or interpreted as mastery in 30/90 days. Time, existing depth, equipment, rights and review access remain unknown.

For paid seats, observe funded starts/exits, contractor hours, transfers, accepted volume, renewal prices and total delivery cost. More output, risk, investment, engineering share or formal responsibility cannot substitute for those outcomes. Hourly, fixed-price, transaction, managed-service and internal-team models allocate savings differently; fixed price is not automatically outcome pricing. Leadership would require sustained accepted decisions, maintenance, others' adoption and mentoring under real constraints, beyond any short project or certificate.

The companion JSON retains **12 exact role snapshots**, source-time states/unknowns, **24 labelled scenario mechanisms**, one existing proof route per bundle, reversal evidence and source-family limits. Validation checks IDs/references and frozen input hashes. This is a concrete synthesis to challenge before the root selects or personalizes any path.
