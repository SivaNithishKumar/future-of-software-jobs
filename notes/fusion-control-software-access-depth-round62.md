# Neural-emulator plasma control: a physical MAST-U feasibility result and a separate software-access boundary

Round62 is held for a different reviewer/root disposition. The question was frozen before requests: what actually ran on a tokamak,under what state,target,shot and safeguard conditions,and what software layer is reachable? The earlier nuclear4 note contained abstract-level pointers; this pass audits the active experiment and its companion integration methods rather than revisiting nuclear markets,OpenMC or archive metadata.

The answer has a strong positive. [Amorisco et al.,*Real-time virtual circuits for plasma shape control via neural network emulators:experimental demonstration on MAST Upgrade*,arXiv2608.28468v1,28August2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.28468v1), reports active feedback control of real MAST-U plasmas using learned response models. This is not just replay,simulation or a future company announcement. It is also deliberately a feasibility study: the authors explicitly say that machine time did not permit a systematic superiority comparison against conventional virtual circuits. No netenergy,commercial reactor,safety certification,operating-cost or developer-seat conclusion follows.

This is a hybrid architecture. Neural emulators predict seven geometric shape descriptors from12activepoloidal-fieldcoil currents,totalplasmacurrent and current-density-profile parameters. Their finite-difference derivatives generate a local response matrix; its pseudoinverse supplies virtual circuits to the existing controller. Selected entries can change by shape parameter/pulsephase. Existing reconstruction,gains,feedback/current-control framework and machine-protection handling remain. The method automates local linearisation updates that otherwise require expert preparation of phased schedules; it does not learn an online reinforcement policy or replace every control layer.

Important state is still prescribed or omitted. Profile parameters come from prior representative discharges rather than live inferred profiles,and induced passive-structure currents are absent. The authors' synthetic equilibrium library spans substantial operating space,but its exact size,training exposure,tuning and campaign-heldout manifest are not audited by this paper pair. Reusing the same trained emulators across the selected scenarios is positive evidence against scenario-specific retraining; it is not a zero-expert-setup or unseen-tokamak claim. Experts still prepare the referencepulse,profiles,targetwaveforms,selected parameters,inverseconfiguration and enabledphases.

The active paper illustrates four discharges; they are not a recovered denominator for all attempted shots:

- **53996:** routine,750kA,double-null reference configuration; three core parameters controlled by RTVC while a conventional VC handles the nose. It reaches programmed duration with close tracking.
- **54000:** requested core perturbations and divertor-leg sweep are tracked before a Langmuir-probe protection trip at563ms. The authors do not attribute this termination to RTVC; possible real-time reconstruction bias is discussed. The trip is retained,not counted as full planned completion or an independently proven controller fault.
- **54002:** requested elongation/nearSuper-X transition is enacted,then nose interaction causes loss of control around380ms. The nose was not directly constrained and pre-shot simulation did not reproduce the interaction. OfflineEFIT++corroborates the preceding transition; that does not make the rest of the shot a success.
- **54168:** all seven parameters,an unusual cross-coupled stress configuration,reach programmed1s ramp-down with substantial transient tracking departures. Poor inverse conditioning is a plausible contributor,not a proven causal explanation.

The same paper discusses missing-state and conditioning limitations explicitly. Alternative reduced diagnostic inversions were offline analyses,not controllers that ran on that shot. Regularized inversion and richer state are proposed future development,not already validated improvements. A bounded hardware success can coexist with informative limitations; neither must be erased.

[Marshall et al.,the companion integration/testing preprint,arXiv2608.26216v1,26August2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.26216v1), separates development tests,replay and three non-actuating commissioning shots from active control. It clarifies the5–5.6ms VC update interval; the activeHTML concatenates the two endpoints. Shape feedback itself runs10kHz using the latest VC. A0.93ms p99 request/output result belongs to an eight-model inference-server test on a similar development machine,not the complete physical sensor-to-actuator loop or an inference on every100µs step. Offline-command agreement and piggyback logging are implementation-consistency evidence,not additional learned-control experiments or physical-model guarantees. Machine limits clip requests; an automatic stale-NN rollback/fault-recovery contract was not recovered.
