## What the randomized positive means

This is stronger than a before/after association: randomized rollout supplies a basis for a causal estimate of **offering the implementation package**, conditional on the design and analysis assumptions. Open-label self-report, period adjustment and selected willing users constrain interpretation; they do not turn randomized evidence into “just correlation” or prove benefit absent. Practitioner well-being is itself a meaningful human outcome, even though it is distinct from patient health.

Three sequences describe allocation timing. The reported22 per sequence is the **power calculation target**, not independently checked realized flow counts. The indexed figure description says1:1 while main methods say1:1:1; the original figure and protocol are needed before treating that text mismatch as an allocation defect. It does not justify discarding the study.

The71,487 notes are repeated observations within66 practitioners, not that many randomized patients or independent clinicians. The27,092/71,487 fraction is the share of **all study notes**, including control time; it is not automatically post-license uptake. Seven low-fidelity users belong in the ITT availability estimand. A comparison restricted to enthusiastic users would answer a different, potentially selection-confounded question.

The95% interval above zero for fulfillment andP=.04 do not contradict the paper’s nonsignificant conclusion: two coprimary outcomes split the error budget. Failure to meet that threshold is not proof of zero benefit. Conversely, a sensitive after-hours secondary result must not be presented as equally robust as the exhaustion or note-time finding; its sensitivity does not erase the other outcomes.

## Measurement, human work and unclosed assumptions

The time unit is a reported standardized8-hour work/patient-time basis, not seconds per encounter or an unconditional whole-day saving. The exact audit-log normalization and capture boundaries require the unavailable appendix/code. **UCLA’s previously noted vendor-editing omission is not evidence of the same omission here.** Whole-workflow interpretation would need coverage of reviewing, correcting, training, support and off-EHR work to be demonstrated, rather than assumed absent or present.

Transcript-grounded draft scores assess a limited reference relationship. They are not an independently adjudicated patient-safety outcome, clinical decision-support evaluation or noninferiority test against physician care. Billing compliance and diagnosis coding also differ from improved diagnoses or health. Do not infer general safety from a favorable average score, or infer harm merely because downstream endpoints were not the trial’s purpose.

The indexed methods describe complete-case survey analysis, practitioner random effects, calendar-period adjustment and specialty stratification. The reported329 surveys are numerically consistent with330 possible five-wave responses; that arithmetic is not a missing-data reanalysis. Attrition/flow, crossover/contamination, update history, code implementation and all subgroup details remain unverified. The article reports registration before enrollment, but the failed registry read cannot establish exact original entries or amendments. SAPv2’s date after the reported study period does not itself prove changed endpoints, selective analysis or misconduct; it also cannot substitute for a checked earlier prespecification history.

Ordinary documentation/EHR workflow is the actual comparator. Optimized templates, speech recognition, human scribes or other vendors are not a measured equal-cost head-to-head comparison here. Training, IT help and operational monitoring are part of the deployed package. The evidence does not isolate a single model’s contribution or establish complete financial return, version stability in every setting or a permanent custom-software need.
