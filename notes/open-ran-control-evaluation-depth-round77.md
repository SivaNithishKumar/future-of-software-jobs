This pass follows the distinct ColO-RAN experiment. It is historical evidence, not a newly released 2026 performance result.

The official record and PDF identify **arXiv:2112.09559v2, 12 January 2022**, by Polese, Bonati, D’Oro, Basagni and Melodia. Its later journal citation does not establish byte or method equivalence to the version read. [Primary version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.09559).

The paper’s key factual projection is retained in the companion JSON, with exact section/figure locators. Its strongest positive is closed-loop actuation on a radio testbed, rather than a predictor scored only against a stored trace. Its limited physical continuation also matters; neither positive should be erased merely because the environment is controlled. [Methods and results, §§3–6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.09559).

Three distinctions govern interpretation. First, a hardware radio passing through an emulated propagation channel is different from both a software-only simulator and a commercial outdoor network. A small indoor over-the-air continuation adds evidence at its own scale; it does not supply broad mobility, vendor or deployment coverage. The printed Fig12/§6 model labels also leave a matched-starting-agent ablation unverified; the plotted gain is not an isolated causal estimate of extra online training. Second, offline transition-data training and online actuation are separate phases. Adaptation after exposure to a changed condition supports an adaptation claim, rather than an untouched zero-shot generalisation claim. Third, the native schedulers and fixed slicing comparison are real positive baselines. An adaptive learned scheduling comparator must not be described as a non-AI baseline; nor is the selected static allocation an exhaustive optimum over all possible heuristics.

The reported outputs are not interchangeable. Throughput, queues, transport blocks and resource-allocation ratios do not directly establish end-user delay, a deadline SLA, rare outage probability, energy savings or operator economics. Collection duration is not a count of successful independent closed-loop trials. Training steps are not elapsed time, especially with unequal parallelism. The selected exploration results also expose a service-performance cost, making a production-safety claim particularly dependent on an independently specified operating policy. No all-attempt failure denominator, calibrated full-loop timing bound, full cost or operational fallback contract was recovered here. Those are precise limits on the claim, not assertions that the study has no value or that all implementations lack safeguards.
