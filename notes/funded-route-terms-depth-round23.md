## What the new official reading establishes

The [public example MoU](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/request/sample_MoU.pdf) links a project plan to reserved support. Its annex describes published, verifiable task outcomes and calls out external interactions; final donations follow verified milestones or previously agreed partial deliveries. Publication, technical correctness, funder verification and upstream merge are therefore distinct events. The example's GPLv3 and reporting clauses belong to that example. The Guide expressly says each MoU is specific, while the general call requires recognized open results; neither document selects a licence or final acceptance test for this developer. Discussed replacement work remains distinct from approval. The referenced amendment policy, version 1.0 of February 2022, records written confirmation and sign-off under its review rules.
