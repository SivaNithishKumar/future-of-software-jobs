# MASAI: a narrow patient-endpoint methods follow-up

The new closure is the reported primary endpoint and20%noninferiority margin, not a new trial or a statistically proven interval-cancer reduction. The [final MASAI abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41620232/) reports a protocol-defined analysis. Its fuller protocol and statistical-plan provenance remain unchecked.

Women were allocated1:1 to AI-assisted triage/detection support with one or two radiologists, or ordinary double reading withoutAI. It reports105,934randomised women and19analysis exclusions. Subtracting these gives105,915; this arithmetic does not prove complete follow-up. Rates are per1,000participants, not person-years. The interval-cancer definition concerns diagnosis between rounds or within two years after a last scheduledscreen, not verified identical observation time for everyone.

Reported ratio0.88(95%CI0.65–1.18) is consistent with noninferiority against a20%relative allowance; its interval includes1, so the comparison does not establish a reduction. Exact test construction, power and analysis populations were not read. Higher reported sensitivity and equal rounded specificity are real positive screening results. Descriptive stage/phenotype counts do not establish a confirmed population stage shift or survival benefit; multiplicity and the full recall/false-positive burden need the analysis plan.

This was human-assisted screening, not autonomous diagnosis. Reduced screen-reading workload cannot be converted into whole-clinical-time savings, staffing or cost without its denominator and operating ledger. No mortality, universal hospital/vendor benefit or developer-job estimate follows.

Two discovery queries and three official body routes were used. Lund’s [official project page](https://portal.research.lu.se/en/projects/mammography-screening-with-artificial-intelligence/) lists dated protocols and a statistical plan. Registry history, the later protocol and SAP were not opened. Date-labelled filenames do not verify their contents. The project’s separately planned additional round through December2026 must not be merged into the completed paper’s follow-up.
