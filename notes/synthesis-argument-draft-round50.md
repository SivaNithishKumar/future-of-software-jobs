# Preliminary synthesis argument — not the final deliverable

This is an integration draft for later skeptical review. It introduces no new evidence, ranking, learning allocation or claim of personal readiness. Source freshness and the final conclusion/actionability review remain locked until 20 October 2026 at 17:30 UTC. Each branch's source-local qualifications control any shorter wording here.

The preparation strategy I would test is to anchor the reader's breadth in one system, one domain and one sustained artifact. Breadth then helps connect software, models, measurements and operating constraints; depth supplies evidence that he can resolve a particular problem. This is a decision under uncertainty, not an observed return to training or a guarantee that the resulting skill will remain scarce in 2031.

The important change is the unit of work being judged. Producing a plausible patch, model output or report is increasingly different from getting a legitimate change accepted and maintained. Existing tools already automate substantial parts of checking, orchestration and diagnosis too. “Learn evaluation” therefore cannot serve as a blanket career moat. The practical question is whether a contribution still improves a particular owner's outcome after competent native tools, review, retries, setup, support and integration are counted.

The map should let the reader explore five connected kinds of work, without turning them into five simultaneous curricula:

* Software platforms, data and delegated actions: make a bounded workflow complete correctly, preserve identity and state through failure, and establish who may cause which effect. A controlled local artifact tests these ideas; it does not establish production authority, customer value or a consulting market.
* Compute, compilers and device systems: understand the actual resource or semantic bottleneck, compare against the maintained implementation and explain the result. Hardware access, numerical tolerance, workload choice and runtime measurement matter. A kernel score or compiler rewrite is not automatically a useful deployment or an accessible job.
* Robotics, control and physical operations: connect sensing, estimation, timing, action and observed effect. Simulation and analytic examples are useful stages. Calibration, intervention, hardware safety and field outcomes require distinct access and evidence; physical need alone does not create funded software work.
* Scientific and industrial software: preserve references, units, information time, uncertainty and domain-specific acceptance. Genomics comparison, lab platforms, grid data, water and crop reporting offer different technical contracts, not interchangeable “AI for science” projects. Native systems and domain credentials remain serious constraints and alternatives.
* Human-facing workflows and creative tools: establish task completion, interaction state, accessible behavior, interchange meaning and usable handoff. A visually convincing output, scanner pass or symbolic fixture alone does not prove those outcomes. Domain practice and a representative reviewer matter, and access to them is not assumed.

Quantum and nuclear work fit into precise software layers within this landscape. A compiler/toolchain role, a decoder experiment, engineering desktop software and a physical research appointment have different prerequisites. A small exploration can reveal whether the reader enjoys the methods and can meet the next gate. It cannot compress a specialist qualification or prove competence in another layer.

For the first stage, choose an accessible branch through the existing diagnostic rather than its publicity. Establish the legitimate task, source/data/code rights, exact baseline and an expected outcome before improving anything. Use the branch's original hours and resource limits, preserve failed and incomplete attempts, and distinguish an exposed regression from independent validation. The 90-day stage is conditional progress in the same artifact; TOTAL and ADDITIONAL budgets cannot be mixed or stacked across fields.

Preparation must also test unaided understanding. An assisted output can be useful while leaving a weak mental model. The proposed delayed prediction, explanation and repair checks are ways to investigate that gap within existing budgets. They are unrun designs, not proof of durable learning or a reason to prohibit AI assistance. Upstream and employer AI-use rules apply to their own activities; permission does not transfer between coding, documentation, applications and interviews.

Progress toward influence has observable gates: understand and reproduce a method; diagnose a meaningful discrepancy; produce a contribution a real maintainer or owner finds useful; support it across changes; then help define acceptance, coordinate others and own consequences. Originality, external acceptance and sustained ownership are different achievements. There is no defensible calendar here promising a title, reputation or wage by 2031.

The decision rules should remain sharp. Prefer the competent baseline when it meets the contract. Narrow an inaccessible arm rather than invent access or reject every smaller lawful result. Stop a custom layer when its extra complexity buys no relevant benefit. Treat missing buyer, employer or reviewer access as an unresolved economic or entry gate. Preserve a valid negative result; it can improve the next decision without proving that an entire field has no future.

The final map needs to expose those choices at the leaves: prerequisites, a concrete first task, baseline, oracle, evidence tier, resources, exact institutions and public routes, qualification barriers, continuation/kill criteria and the next accountable contribution. Its size, number of branches and number of citations are not evidence that these questions have been answered.
